## Statement

\[
\Xi(s)=\tfrac12 s(s-1)\pi^{-s/2}\Gamma(s/2)\zeta(s),\qquad \Xi(s)=\Xi(1-s).
\] The zeros of \(\Xi\) are precisely the non-trivial zeros of \(\zeta\).
