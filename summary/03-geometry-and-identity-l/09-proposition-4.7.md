# Proposition 4.7 (Jet excludes multiple off-line balanced closures)

## Statement

Assume \(\varepsilon\in(0,1/6)\) and \(K\in\mathbb{N}\). Let \(\rho\) be a zero of \(\Xi\) of multiplicity \(m\) with \(1\le m\le K\) and \(\sigma\le\tfrac12-\delta\), \(\delta>\varepsilon\). Then no point of the isolation disk of \(\rho\) can satisfy \[
\bigl\lvert\partial_t^{\,m}\eta_N^{(N^2)}(s)\bigr\rvert\le N^{1/2+\varepsilon}(2\log N)^m
\] for all large \(N\) with \(\lvert\gamma\rvert\le N^{1/2-\varepsilon}\). In particular, if balanced closures are required to obey this bound for every \(k=1,\dots,K\), no such \(\rho\) is a persistent balanced atom.

## Proof

Lemma 4.5.3 gives the opposite inequality on \(\partial_t^{\,m}\eta\) throughout the disk. The case \(m=1\) is Proposition 4.6.
