> **Editorial note.** This is the limitative result: without a multiplicative hypothesis, no theorem from the additive toolbox can be RH.

## Statement

No theorem deducible from the ingredients listed in the proof of Theorem C.8 — the Euler-sum pair structure, Euler–Maclaurin or mean-zero partial summation, Jensen, Rouché, the minimum principle, Riemann–von Mangoldt, and a classical floor for the limit off its zeros — can imply that all zeros of \(\zeta\) in \(S_\delta\) lie on \(\operatorname{Re}s=\tfrac12\). Any such deduction would apply verbatim to \(f\) and contradict Lemma C.4(iii).

## Proof

Immediate from Theorem C.8: the hypotheses of any such theorem hold for the twist, and its conclusion fails there.
