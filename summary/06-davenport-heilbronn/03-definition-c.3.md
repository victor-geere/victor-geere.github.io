## Statement

Let \[
\xi=\frac{\sqrt{10-2\sqrt5}-2}{\sqrt5-1}=0.2840790438\ldots,
\] and let \(a^{\mathrm{DH}}\) be the sequence of period \(5\) with \((a_1,\dots,a_5)=(1,\xi,-\xi,-1,0)\). Write \(f(s)=\sum_{n\ge1}a^{\mathrm{DH}}_n n^{-s}\) for its Dirichlet series, entire because the coefficients have mean zero.
