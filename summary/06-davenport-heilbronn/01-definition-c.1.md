## Statement

For \(\omega\in\mathbb{C}\) with \(\omega^q=1\) and real \(x\ge 0\), \[
H_x^{(s)}[\omega]=\sum_{n\le x}\omega^{n}n^{-s},
\] the depth-one finite Euler sum of weight \(s\) at level \(q\) (a truncated Lerch value). For a \(q\)-periodic sequence \(a=(a_r)\) put \(H_x^{(s)}[a]=\sum_{n\le x}a_n n^{-s}\) and \(\widehat{a}_j=q^{-1}\sum_{r=1}^{q}a_r e(-jr/q)\).
