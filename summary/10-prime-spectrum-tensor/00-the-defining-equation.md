# The defining equation $(\star)$

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Type | Conjecture / proposed criterion |
| Status | Refuted (Theorem B) |
| Source | [`tensor.md`](tensor.md), introduction and §4; original statement in [`tensor.html`](tensor.html) §4 |

## Statement

For the current $\mathcal{P}=\Pi+\Theta$ of Definition 3.1 of `tensor.html`, the proposed defining equation is

\[
\bigl\langle\mathcal{P},\,f*\check f\bigr\rangle\;\ge\;0
\qquad\text{for every }f\text{ in the Weil cone},
\tag{\(\star\)}
\]

and the question is whether the zeros of $\zeta$ are the Bochner spectrum of $\mathcal{P}$.

## Verdict

$(\star)$ is **false**, unconditionally, by Theorem B: the witness $f_0$ gives $-0.67730247811\ldots$. The failure is produced by the *on-line* zeros, so it carries no information about the Riemann hypothesis. The statement on $\mathbb{R}$ that is equivalent to RH is Weil positivity for the repaired current $\mathcal{W}$, not $(\star)$ for $\mathcal{P}$.
