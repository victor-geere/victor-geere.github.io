## Statement and proof

For \(g\) in the cone,
\[
\langle\mathcal{W},g\rangle\;:=\;2\int_{\mathbb{R}}g(u)\cosh\tfrac u2\,du\;-\;2\,\Pi(g)\;+\;\frac1{2\pi}\int_{\mathbb{R}}\hat g(t)\,\Omega(t)\,dt .
\]
