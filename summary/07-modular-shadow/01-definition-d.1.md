## Statement

Let \(\mathcal{A}=\bigl(\prod_p\mathbb{F}_p\bigr)/\bigl(\bigoplus_p\mathbb{F}_p\bigr)\), the Kaneko–Zagier ring [\[19\]](publication.html#ref-kz), whose elements are families \((x_p)_p\) modulo those with \(x_p=0\) for almost all \(p\). For a \(q\)-periodic sequence \(a\) with entries in the ring of integers \(\mathcal{O}_K\) of a number field \(K\), and an integer weight \(k\ge1\), set \[
F_{\mathcal{A}}[a](k)=\Bigl(\ \sum_{n=1}^{p-1}a_n\,n^{-k}\ \bmod\ \mathfrak{p}\ \Bigr)_{\mathfrak{p}},
\] the family indexed by primes \(\mathfrak{p}\) of \(K\) of residue degree one. For \(a\in\mathbb{Z}^q\) this lies in \(\mathcal{A}\); in general it lies in the analogue \(\mathcal{A}_K\) built from \(\mathcal{O}_K/\mathfrak{p}\), and depends on the choice of \(\mathfrak{p}\) above \(p\). The Galois trace over the conjugates is choice-free.
