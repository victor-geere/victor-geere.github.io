## Statement

Addendum E adds no hypothesis and re-proves nothing. Its dependencies are Theorem 4.3, Lemma 2.2 and Lemma 3.2 from the body, Theorems C.7, C.9 and C.12 from Addendum C, and the argument principle and Rouché's theorem from Ahlfors [\[4\]](publication.html#ref-ahlfors). It is used nowhere in Sections 1–14, and Corollaries C.10 and C.14 are unaffected by it.

The three objects it introduces line up with three objects already in the paper. The straightening angle \(\psi_\eta\) is the phase whose derivative in \(t\) drives the slow-passage condition of Definition 5.1 and Identity L; the gain \(\varrho\) is \(\lvert\eta\rvert/\lvert s\rvert\), the modulus that the closure tests of Definition 4.4 threshold; and the punctures of the field are the atoms of the limiting measures of Section 12. Corollary E.5(ii) and Corollary 12.3 are two encodings of RH in the same construction, one by holonomy and one by support.
