## Statement

Any weak-star limit point \(\nu\) of \(\{\nu_N\}\) is a tempered pure-point measure on \(S_\delta\) whose support consists of non-trivial zeros of \(\Xi\). Every critical-line zero is an atom of \(\nu\). A simple zero with \(\sigma\le\tfrac12-\varepsilon\) is not: Marty speed fails (Lemma 4.8), so the left half-strip is absent from both \(\operatorname{supp}\nu\) and \(\operatorname{supp}\mu\). A simple zero with \(\sigma\ge\tfrac12+\varepsilon/2\) is an unrestricted atom and not a balanced one.

## Proof

The identification of atoms with zeros is Theorem 10.1. Existence at a critical-line zero is Theorem 5.2. Existence on the right and exclusion on the left are Lemma 4.8 together with the relative clause of Theorem 5.2; the grid miss is controlled by \(\kappa=\varepsilon\) and the logarithmic spacing refinement of Definition 5.1. The comparison of the two supports is Remark 5.3.
