## Statement

On the critical line, \[
\partial_R\eta=A(R)+B(R)+E(R),
\] where \(A=\sum_{m\le R^2}(\partial_R c_m)m^{-1/2-i\gamma}\) is the interior coefficient derivative, \(B=c_{\lfloor R^2\rfloor}(R^2)^{-1/2-i\gamma}\cdot 2R\) is the Leibniz boundary, and \(E\) is the difference between this sharp splitting and a smooth cutoff of width \(R^{2\theta}\), \(\theta\in(0,1/4)\). Since \(\lvert(R^2)^{-1/2}\rvert=R^{-1}\), one has \(\lvert B\rvert=2\lvert c_{\lfloor R^2\rfloor}\rvert\). For the integer filter, \(N\mid N^2\), so \(c_{N^2}=1-N\) and \(\lvert B\rvert=2(N-1)\). The integer theorems of Sections 4–5 and 8–12 do not use \(B\).
