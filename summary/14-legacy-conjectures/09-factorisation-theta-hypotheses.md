# Factorisation-theta hypotheses

**Programme:** theta functions and divisor factorisation essays.
**Source:** [`factorisation-theta-zeta-2.html`](archive/essays/factorisation-theta-zeta-2.html), §9.
**Status:** speculative; no precise theorem.

## Statements

The essay lists:

- **Hypothesis 1.** $F(s)$ or $Z(s)$ encodes $D_k(s)$ via analytic continuation.
- **Hypothesis 2.** $\theta_f(t)$ has modular properties tied to $k$.
- **Open question.** Can $f(x)$ generate $\sigma(k)$ directly?

Here $f(x)=-\cos(2\pi x)-\cos(2\pi k/x)+2$ (or a close variant) has double zeros at the divisors of $k$, and $D_k(s)$ is the associated divisor Dirichlet series.

## Status

These are exploratory hypotheses in an early essay, not definitions precise enough to prove or refute. They are recorded for completeness because the archive explicitly labels them. They are not used by the canonical paper and have no role in the RH programme.
