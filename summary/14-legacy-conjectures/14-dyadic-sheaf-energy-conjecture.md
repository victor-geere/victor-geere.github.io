# Dyadic-sheaf energy conjecture

**Programme:** dyadic sheaf on the critical strip.
**Source:** [`dyadic-sheaf.html`](archive/essays/rh/dyadic-sheaf.html), step 1 of the speculative pathway.
**Status:** speculative conjecture; no proof.

## Statement

Define the quaternion-lifted divisor detector
$$
\phi(x)=\cos(2\pi x)\mathbf{i}+\cos(2\pi p/x)\mathbf{j},
$$
and the energy
$$
E(x;t,\sigma)=[1-\cos(2\pi t/x)]^2+[1-\cos(2\pi x)]^2.
$$

**Conjecture.**
$$
\inf_x E(x;t,\sigma)=0
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\sigma=\tfrac12\ \text{and}\ t\ \text{is an ordinate of a non-trivial zero.}
$$

## How the programme uses it

The conjecture is the first step of a speculative pathway: embed the divisor detector into the xi function via Fourier–Mellin duality, introduce a dyadic sheaf on the critical strip, and use sheaf cohomology to show that non-trivial sections exist only on the critical line. The later steps are explicitly labelled speculative.

## Status

Speculative. The essay’s abstract calls the whole pathway speculative and says RH remains unproven. It is not used by the canonical paper. Archived for provenance.
