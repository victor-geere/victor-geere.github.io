# Kösters’ higher-moment conjecture

**Programme:** sine kernel and shifted moments of $\zeta$.
**Source:** [`kosters-zeta-moments-sinc.html`](archive/essays/kosters-zeta-moments-sinc.html), §5.
**Status:** external open conjecture (Kösters 2008).

## Statement

Kösters proved that the sine kernel appears in the second and fourth shifted moments of $\zeta$. He conjectured (Conjecture 1.3) that the sine kernel appears in **higher even moments** as well, with the asymptotic form predicted by the Conrey–Farmer–Keating–Rubinstein–Snaith (CFKRS) conjecture.

The second-moment theorem quoted in the essay is

$$
\lim_{T\to\infty}\frac{1}{T\log T}\int_0^T
\zeta\Bigl(\tfrac12+it+\frac{\pi\mu}{\log t}\Bigr)
\zeta\Bigl(\tfrac12-it+\frac{\pi\nu}{\log t}\Bigr)\,dt
=e^{-i\pi(\mu-\nu)}\frac{\sin(\pi(\mu-\nu))}{\pi(\mu-\nu)}.
$$

## Status

This is a conjecture in the analytic number theory literature, not a result of the site. The essay records it as part of the sine-kernel background. It is not used by the canonical paper.
