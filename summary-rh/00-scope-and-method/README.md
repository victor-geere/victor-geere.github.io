# 00 — Scope and method

This folder explains what `summary-rh/` contains, what it leaves out, and how the statements were classified.

## What was harvested

- **The ledger.** `research/truths.md` §§1–13, the audited list of proved results, with 140 named rows and Lean links. Reproduced in [01 — Ledger](summary-rh/01-ledger/README.md).
- **The published papers.** `research/publish/001–015/manuscript.md`, every bold theorem/lemma/proposition/corollary/conjecture/hypothesis/definition block. 147 statements across 15 papers, in [02 — Papers](summary-rh/02-published-papers/README.md).
- **The open layer.** `research/open.md` OP-1…OP-19 and the 36 harvested OPEN/CONJECTURED items; `research/TODO.md` §A.1 for OP-19; the 2026 out-of-reach reassessment. In [03 — Open problems](summary-rh/03-open-problems/README.md).
- **The programme narrative.** `research/twin-prime-parity/README.md` (verdict, rounds, X1–X6) and `dictionary/05` (P1–P19, B1–B5, N1–N3). In [04 — Programme](summary-rh/04-programme/README.md).
- **Verification and errata.** The Lean audit, status vocabulary, and the consolidated corrections. In [05 — Lean verification](summary-rh/05-lean-verification/README.md).
- **Provenance.** The round/journal layers, indexed but not reprinted. In [06 — Archive](summary-rh/06-archive/README.md).

## What was excluded

- The 38,751-line `research-journal.md` and the 18,706-line `open/HANDOFF.md` — session logs, indexed only.
- The 39 `theorems/rh3–rh39` folders and 39 `html/rh*` papers — duplicates of the ledger/papers/open register.
- Lean sources and Python scripts — indexed via the status/paper files, not reprinted.
- `xi-horn/`, `lean/`, `lib/`, `adv/`, `chowla2/`, `odd-core-adversary/`, `verify/` — supporting code/numerics.
- `various/` — pre-programme reconnaissance and the A/B dialogue.

The full rule is in [Scope](summary-rh/00-scope-and-method/scope.md).

## The headline

The programme’s verdict is **no**: the greedy harmonic sine does not solve the parity problem for the twin prime conjecture. It produced one clean new theorem — the finite-prefix indeterminacy theorem — and a complete map of the barrier. It did **not** prove RH, the twin prime conjecture, Chowla, Sarnak, or Elliott.
