## Duplicates and editions

- **001 / 011** are the same paper. 011 is the packaging of truths §11; 001 is the first edition.
- **007 / 014** are the same theorem. 014 is the fuller edition (self-contained HTML paper, independent recomputation, logs).
- **013** is the registry/synthesis; it overlaps 001–012 but adds the O1–O3 objectives and the explicit-function programme.

## Status

Each paper is published/closed as a paper. “Published” does **not** mean every claim is proved: 007/014 explicitly leave two-point Chowla open; 008 has no Lean layer; 015 carries a correction notice. The per-paper files mark the harvested statements and their source text.
