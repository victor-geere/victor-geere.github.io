Geometric & Harmonic Decomposition Lab

Set A and set B → geometric (binary) carries, harmonic (Egyptian) carries, and real-number harmonic expansions. Exact rational arithmetic; all three panels are slider-driven.

How to read this lab (interpretation, stated up front)

1 · Geometric. Each integer a is written as a · Σn≥1 1/2n. Grouping by denominator gives S copies of every 1/2n where S = ΣA + ΣB (or per set). Since Σn≥2 1/2n = 1/2, the whole tail of smaller fractions merges into S extra halves, leaving 2S copies of 1/2 — the largest fraction allowed when 1/1 is excluded. (Allow 1/1 and the canonical form collapses to S copies of 1/1.)

2 · Harmonic. Each integer is expanded by the greedy Egyptian (harmonic) algorithm with distinct denominators ≥ 2: repeatedly subtract the largest unit fraction that fits. The expansions from all elements are pooled, grouped by denominator, then merged: sub-multisets that sum to a unit fraction are replaced by it, always preferring the largest fraction (smallest denominator). Denominators grow fast (Sylvester-like), so the term cap matters; the exact remainder is shown.

3 · Real numbers. Same greedy harmonic expansion, applied to real values, with no duplicates within a number. Because merging two terms would create a duplicate, no merging is performed per number. Rational inputs terminate exactly; the term slider shows the partial construction and the remainder.
