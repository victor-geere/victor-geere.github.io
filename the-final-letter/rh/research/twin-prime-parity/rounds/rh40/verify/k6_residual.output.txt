
==============================================================================
§1  the correct u-map   (u = 1/r, q = ceil(u), s = q - u)
==============================================================================
  claimed in the brief:  u_{k+1} = u^2/(u - floor(u) + 1)
  derived here:          u_{k+1} = u q/(q - u) = q^2/s - q
  also exact:            s_{k+1} = 1 - {q^2/s},  q = u + s

  dps = 400; every deviation below is measured against 1/r' where
  r' is produced by the *squaring* form of the map.

  target       steps     max |u_map - 1/r_next|     max |brief - 1/r_next|  max |u-(q-s)|/u
  1/pi             7               7.98804e-202                   0.785165     6.918e-202
  log 2            8               8.35307e-202                   0.935315     7.072e-202
  sqrt2-1          8               1.24745e-201                   0.925123     6.304e-202
  e-2              8               6.80321e-202                   0.985998     5.708e-202
  sqrt3-1          8               1.24434e-201                    0.96827     6.997e-202
  log2/log10       7               7.26238e-202                   0.934974     3.035e-202

  => the derived map is exact to the working precision; the brief's
     candidate is off by a relative error of order q (not a typo-level
     discrepancy).  Note also q = u + s and u_{k+1} = u_k q_k/s_k.

  the Gauss map on the fractional part would be f_{k+1} = { 1/f_k }.

§5  the true statement: -1 for a.e. x  (L^1 PROVED, a.e. CONJECTURE)
==============================================================================
  Sylvester sequence 2,3,7,43,1807,... bounds the greedy digits from
  below, so sum_k 1/q_k <= sum_k 1/S_k = 1 exactly (telescoping).
  Partial sum of the first 8 terms = 1.0;  tail < 1/(S_9-1) ~ 1e-38.

  By the exact conditional law (docstring (D)):  E[log s_k] = -1
  + (1/2)E[1/q_k] + O(E[1/q_k^2]), and sum_k E[1/q_k] <= 1.  Hence
  E[(1/K) sum log s_k] = -1 + O(1/K): L^1 convergence to -1, proved.
  The a.e. version is reduced in docstring (H) to the expanding-map
  correlation lemma, which is stated but not proved here.

  Orbit evidence for generic x.  The exact conditional mean of
  log s_k is -1 + 1/(2q_k) + O(1/q_k^2) (section 2), so the sum is
  -K + O(1): the running mean sits at -1 with fluctuations of order
  1/sqrt(K).  Crucially the individual log s_k are O(1) throughout --
  they do not decay, so the sum cannot converge.

SUMMARY
==============================================================================
  u-map (verified):        u' = u ceil(u)/(ceil(u) - u);  brief's
                           u^2/(u-floor(u)+1) is refuted.
  fractional-part map:     s' = 1 - {q^2/s},  f' = {q^2/(1-f)}; a skew
                           product, NOT Gauss; invariant density ->
                           Lebesgue, not Gauss-Kuzmin.
  correction term:         PROVED convergent; the problem is sum log s_k.
  K6 as posed:             REFUTED.  Explicit irrational x realise the
                           limit log s* for every s* in (0,1]; another
                           family has no limit at all.
  replaced by:             (1/K) sum log c_k -> -1 in L^1 (PROVED), and
                           a.e. (CONJECTURE modulo the stated
                           expanding-map correlation lemma).
