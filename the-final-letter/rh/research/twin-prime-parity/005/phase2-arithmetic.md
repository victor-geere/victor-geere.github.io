# Phase 2 — no Euler product, and the only functional relation is Mahler

Script: [`phase2_arithmetic.py`](phase2_arithmetic.py). Goal: *does any greedy support
`A(x)` give `D_x(s) = Σ_{k∈A(x)}(k+2)^{−s}` an exact Euler product or a classical
functional equation?* Answer: **no Euler product ever, and the only functional relation is
the Mahler scaling `s → 2s`, which forces a natural boundary.**

## (i) No Euler product

An Euler product `∏_p E_p(s)` expands into a Dirichlet series supported on a
**multiplicatively closed** set of integers. The greedy supports are not:

| support | multiplicatively closed? | witness |
|---|---|---|
| `{2}` | no | `2·2 = 4` missing |
| `{2,3}` | no | `2·2 = 4` missing |
| `{2,3,7}` | no | `2·2 = 4` missing |
| Sylvester `{2,3,7,43,1807,…}` | no | `2·2 = 4` missing |

The only multiplicatively closed finite support would be a singleton `{m}`, giving the
monomial `m^{−s}` — a single local factor with no arithmetic content.

For irrational `x` non-multiplicativity is **forced, not incidental**. Sylvester sparsity
gives `n_{j+1} ≥ n_j(n_j−1)+1`, i.e. essentially squaring: `n_6 = 32 634 043` (7 digits),
and by `j = 6` in the ladder used here the support already has `14`-digit elements, i.e.
doubly-exponential growth. A multiplicative semigroup of integers generated by its least
element `m` grows at most like `m^j` — exponentially. A doubly-exponential set can never be
multiplicatively closed. Hence:

> **no greedy support carries an Euler product.** `D_x(s)` is an entire function with no
> arithmetic factorisation.

## (ii) The only functional relation is Mahler-type

The supports are (almost) self-similar under squaring, and the series inherits a scaling
relation:

```
geometric model  n_j = q^{2^j} :   D(s) − D(2s) = q^{−s}   EXACT
true Sylvester ladder          :   D(s) − D(2s) = 2^{−s}(1 + ε(s))
```

Measured `ε(s)` for the true ladder (RELATIVE correction; the ABSOLUTE correction is `~3^{−s}`, see [`closed-form.md`](closed-form.md) §6.5 — an earlier `O(s Σ_j s_j^{−2s−1})` claim was wrong):

| `s` | 1 | 2 | 4 | 8 | 16 | 32 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| ratio `(D(s)−D(2s))/2^{−s}` | 1.235879 | 1.227192 | 1.139258 | 1.035151 | 1.001507 | 1.000002 |
| correction `ε` | 2.36·10⁻¹ | 2.27·10⁻¹ | 1.39·10⁻¹ | 3.52·10⁻² | 1.51·10⁻³ | 2.32·10⁻⁶ |

So the Mahler equation is exact for the geometric model and asymptotically exact for the
true Sylvester ladder — but **never exactly**, because `s_{j+1} = s_j² − s_j + 1 ≠ s_j²`.

## (iii) Natural boundary — hence no classical functional equation

On `Re s = 0` every term has modulus `|n_j^{−it}| = 1`, for every `j`, at every `t`. The
terms do not tend to `0`, so **the series diverges at every point of the line `Re s = 0`**.
The abscissa of convergence is `0` and that line is a **natural boundary**.
**Corrected (see [`closed-form.md`](closed-form.md) §6.3):** divergence on the line is *not*
sufficient for this — `Σ(−1)^{n−1}n^{−s}` diverges on `Re s = 0` too and continues to
`(1−2^{1−s})ζ(s)`. The correct input is **Fabry's gap theorem**: with `z = e^{−s}`,
`D = Σ_j z^{λ_j}` with `λ_j = ln s_j` and `λ_j/j → ∞`, so `|z| = 1` is a natural boundary.
Iterating the
Mahler relation, `D(s) = Σ_{k<K} n_0^{−2^k s} + D(2^K s)`, propagates the divergence
outwards, so no analytic continuation across `Re s = 0` exists.

A classical (Riemann-type) functional equation is precisely a device for meromorphic
continuation to a full plane. Since none can exist here, the Mahler relation is the *only*
functional structure — and its consequence is a boundary rather than a symmetry.

## Verdict on target 2

Target 2 of `monotone-analogue.md` §6 — *"a greedy-support series with an exact functional
equation or Euler product for some explicit `x`"* — is **refuted**:

- Euler product: impossible for every `x`, except the degenerate monomials `x = 1/m`;
- functional equation: only the Mahler scaling `s → 2s` exists, and it is non-arithmetic;
  it produces a natural boundary, so no classical functional equation can hold.

doubly-exponential vs exponential growth argument; the exact Mahler relation for the
geometric model; divergence on `Re s = 0` (natural boundary). NUMERICAL: the
