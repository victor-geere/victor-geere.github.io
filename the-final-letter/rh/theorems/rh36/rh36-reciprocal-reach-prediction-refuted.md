# A prediction of mine, refuted: the threshold is not the reciprocal of the measure's reach

**Kind:** Claim
**Status:** REFUTED (prediction, measured over five windows)
**Area:** rh-html
**Source:** [`rh36.html`](../../html/rh36.html) — §3 "The threshold is a crossing", boxed

## Statement

> **A prediction of mine, refuted.** Before measuring the crossing I expected the threshold to be the reciprocal of the measure's reach — a displacement is invisible until δ·γ ≈ 1 — which predicts that δ×γ is constant across windows. Over five windows γ runs from 91 to 1099, a factor 12.1, and the product runs 0.459 to 2.229, a factor 4.9. It is not the bandwidth. It is the crossing, and a crossing is a property of the matrix rather than of the measure.

## Hypotheses and notation

- δ is the displacement of the rates (rh35's slide), γ the ordinate scale; the predicted invariant is δ×γ.
- The prediction: a displacement is invisible until δ·γ ≈ 1, so δ×γ would be constant across windows.
- Five windows are measured, γ from 91 to 1099.

## Proof / evidence

Numerical test of the registered prediction: γ spans a factor 12.1 and the product δ×γ runs 0.459 to 2.229, a factor 4.9, so the invariant fails.
