# What replaced it: the repaired two-parameter conjecture

**Kind:** Lemma
**Status:** CONJECTURED (exact on the crystal; obeyed within a factor 1.9 on constructed voids)
**Area:** rh-html
**Source:** [`rh32.html`](../../html/rh32.html) — §7 "The conjunction", lines 1159–1174 and boxed statement at lines 1163–1174

## Statement

> **What replaced it.** The repaired conjecture is two-parameter: for a mirrored atomic measure whose widest thin arc spans K kernel wavelengths at resolution n,
>
> λ<sub>min</sub> ≥ C<sub>n</sub>·c(K),   c(K) = (π<sup>2</sup>K/2)(1 − l<sub>1</sub>(K)) —
>
> exact on the crystal by the identity of §2; obeyed by every constructed void on zeta material within a factor 1.9 (§4); and vacuously safe on rh31's whole grid, where K never exceeds 1.45 and c(1.45) is still order one. For zeta windows the hypothesis "K stays small" is not an assumption to add — it is what rh31 §6 measured: wrapped ordinates equidistribute, so the material cannot build the void that kills it.

## Hypotheses and notation

- The measure is mirrored and atomic; its widest thin arc spans K kernel wavelengths at resolution n (K = nW/2π).
- C<sub>n</sub> is the emptiest-arc Fejér density; c(K) = (π<sup>2</sup>K/2)(1 − l<sub>1</sub>(K)) is [rh32-death-of-the-constant](rh32-death-of-the-constant.md)'s measured function.
- l<sub>1</sub>(K) is the top concentration eigenvalue of the void's operator.

## Proof / evidence

No proof. The source states that the concentration step is the classical prolate theory (measured to 6.2%, exact on the crystal) and names the one remaining gap: "the quadrature step — the atoms dominate a smoothed density on degree-n forms, so that the crystal computation transfers — is exact on the crystal (§2, one line) and is the single remaining analytic gap for real configurations: a one-sided Marcinkiewicz–Zygmund statement for Poisson-band point sets, loss bounded by a function of the gap statistic alone."
