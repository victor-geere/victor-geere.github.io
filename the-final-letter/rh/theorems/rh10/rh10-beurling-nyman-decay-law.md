# The Beurling–Nyman decay law d_N² ~ (2+γ_E−ln 4π)/ln N

**Kind:** Claim
**Status:** CONJECTURED (law cited; measured here)
**Area:** rh-html
**Source:** [`rh10.html`](../../html/rh10.html) — §4 "The ruler: writing 1 with fractional parts", equation (5) and following

## Statement

> The Gram system then gives
>
> d₁ = 0.5606, &nbsp; d₂ = 0.4160, &nbsp; d₃ = 0.2745, &nbsp; … , &nbsp; d₂₀ = 0.1269,
>
> strictly decreasing, and tracking the conjectured law d<sub>*N*</sub>² ~ (2+γ<sub>E</sub>−ln 4π)/ln *N* (Báez-Duarte–Balazard–Landreau–Saias; Burnol): measured d₂₀² ln 20 = 0.0482 against the limit 0.0462.

## Hypotheses and notation

- d<sub>*N*</sub> is the L² distance of [the Beurling–Nyman criterion](rh10-beurling-nyman-criterion.md), equation (4).
- γ<sub>E</sub> is Euler's constant; the limit constant is 2+γ<sub>E</sub>−ln 4π = 2λ₁ = 0.0462 (twice the value of [the λ₁ identity](rh10-lambda-one-three-dials.md) in §5).
- The source calls the law "conjectured" and attributes it to Báez-Duarte–Balazard–Landreau–Saias and Burnol.

## Proof / evidence

The source gives no proof of the law; it reports the computed distances d₁…d₂₀ (strictly decreasing, by exact piecewise integration of the Gram system) and the comparison d₂₀² ln 20 = 0.0482 against the limit 0.0462. It notes the convergence is logarithmic, so no numerical run approaches 0.

## Dependencies and related results

- [The Beurling–Nyman criterion: RH ⟺ d_N → 0](rh10-beurling-nyman-criterion.md) — the criterion this law quantifies.
- [The one constant on three dials](rh10-lambda-one-three-dials.md) — λ₁, whose double is the conjectured decay constant.
- rh5 §6 is the source's earlier treatment of the same problem.

## Notes

- The law is stated by the source as conjectural ("the conjectured law"), and the harvested status says so; the measurement 0.0482 vs 0.0462 is a finite-*N* comparison, not a verification of the asymptotic.
- The source's reading: "The convergence is logarithmic, so no numerical run approaches 0 — the point of the instrument is different."
