# [GOR⁺] Corollary 1.3 — hyperbolicity for d ≤ 9 × 10²⁴

**Kind:** Corollary
**Status:** CITED (literature — Griffin, Ono, Rolen, Thorner, Tripp, Wagner, Adv. Math. **397** (2022) 108186; conditional on Platt–Trudgian's verified height)
**Area:** xi-horn

## Statement

> **[GOR⁺] Corollary 1.3.** *If d ≤ 9 × 10²⁴ and n ≥ 0, then J_{d,n}(X) is hyperbolic.*

## Hypotheses and notation

- J_{d,n}(X) = Σ_{j=0}^{d} C(d,j)·γ(n+j)·X^j ([O'S] (1.3)); γ is the papers' γ(n) = the directory's a(n).
- Hyperbolic = all zeros real; the corollary covers the whole strip d ≤ 9 × 10²⁴ at every shift n ≥ 0.
- 9 × 10²⁴ is Platt–Trudgian's T = 3 000 175 332 800 squared (source §4).

## Proof / evidence

The source records the derivation and its rate: "The corollary's 9×10²⁴ is Platt–Trudgian's T = 3 000 175 332 800, squared." It is the third row of the map: "| d ≤ 9×10²⁴ | all n ≥ 0 | [GOR⁺] Cor. 1.3 | RH verified to height T (Platt–Trudgian), d ≤ T² |".

## Dependencies and related results

- [GOR⁺] Theorem 1.2 ([harvested](xihorn-uniformity-gorplus-theorem-12.md)) is the mechanism; Platt–Trudgian's zero verification is the input.
- It is one of the two rows that "pin the band's outer edge"; the other is [O'S] Theorem 1.3 ([harvested](xihorn-uniformity-osullivan-theorem-13.md)) for the large-n wedge.
- In the companion source: [xihorn-gorz-gap-gorplus-corollary-13.md](xihorn-gorz-gap-gorplus-corollary-13.md).
