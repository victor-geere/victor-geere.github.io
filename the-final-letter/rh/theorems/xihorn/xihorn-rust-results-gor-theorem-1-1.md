# [GOR⁺] Theorem 1.1 — n ≥ c·e^d forces hyperbolicity

**Kind:** Theorem
**Status:** CITED (literature — Griffin–Ono–Rolen–Thorner–Tripp–Wagner, Adv. Math. 397 (2022) 108186)
**Area:** xi-horn
**Source:** [`xi-horn/rust/RESULTS.md`](../../xi-horn/rust/RESULTS.md) — Summary, H3

## Statement

> Both certify through Turán's Theorem III and both bound the onset only
> asymptotically — n ≥ c·e^d for J ([GOR⁺] Thm 1.1, **c never given**) …

## Hypotheses and notation

- The conclusion is hyperbolicity of J^{d,n} once n ≥ c·e^d, c a positive constant.
- **c is never given** in the source.
- Comparison recorded: "the measured J onset grows *more slowly* than e^d over 8 ≤ d ≤ 16, the implied c falling from 0.0171 to 0.0003, so the exponential regime their proof needs has not begun at these degrees."

## Proof / evidence

The source states the bound without proof and without a value of c, and records that it "cannot be compared" with the measured onset for that reason.
