# [O'S] Theorem 1.3 — the exponential made polynomial

**Kind:** Theorem
**Status:** CITED (literature — O'Sullivan, arXiv:2007.13582v2)
**Area:** xi-horn
**Source:** [`xi-horn/H_track.md`](../../xi-horn/H_track.md) — §H3 "where the Turán criteria actually start to hold"

## Statement

> both bound the onset only asymptotically — … and **n/log²n ≥ d^{3/4}/2** for P
> ([O'S] Theorem 1.3, "d large").

That is the whole of the statement the source gives for this theorem; its role in
H3 is as the second published bound the measured onset is compared against.

## Hypotheses and notation

- The condition is n/log²n ≥ d^{3/4}/2, for **d large** — the source quotes the paper's qualifier exactly: "d large".
- It concerns P^{d,n}, the Hermite family ([O'S] (1.4)), the criterion whose Ω = e^{−z²} form is (8.2).

## Proof / evidence

The source states the bound without proof. It records the comparison: "Where both are defined, [O'S] Theorem 1.3's sufficient bound overshoots the true onset by **296× to 1602×**."
