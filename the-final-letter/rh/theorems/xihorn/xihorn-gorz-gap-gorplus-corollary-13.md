# [GOR⁺] Corollary 1.3 — hyperbolicity through d ≤ 9 × 10²⁴

**Kind:** Corollary
**Status:** CITED (literature — Griffin, Ono, Rolen, Thorner, Tripp, Wagner, Adv. Math. **397** (2022) 108186; conditional on Platt–Trudgian's verification of RH₀(3 000 175 332 800))
**Area:** xi-horn
**Source:** [`xi-horn/gorz-gap.md`](../../xi-horn/gorz-gap.md) — §1.3 "The exception sets, and the gap", quoted theorem box; also quoted in [`xi-horn/uniformity-in-the-jensen-degree-d.md`](../../xi-horn/uniformity-in-the-jensen-degree-d.md) §4

## Statement

> **[GOR⁺] Corollary 1.3.** *If d ≤ 9 × 10²⁴ and n ≥ 0, then J_{d,n}(X) is hyperbolic.*

## Hypotheses and notation

- J_{d,n}(X) = Σ_{j=0}^{d} C(d,j)·a(n+j)·X^j (equation (4)); hyperbolic = all zeros real.
- The whole strip d ≤ 9 × 10²⁴ is covered at every shift n ≥ 0; equivalently E(d) = ∅ for d ≤ 9 × 10²⁴.
- 9 × 10²⁴ is 3 000 175 332 800² — Platt–Trudgian's verified height T squared (the source prints "3 000 175 332 800² = 9.001 × 10²⁴").

## Proof / evidence

The source gives the derivation, verbatim: "(Corollary 1.3 is Theorem 1.2 fed with Platt–Trudgian's verification of RH₀(3 000 175 332 800); 3 000 175 332 800² = 9.001 × 10²⁴. …)"

The companion source §4 records the same: "the corollary's 9×10²⁴ is Platt–Trudgian's T = 3 000 175 332 800, squared. Covering all d at that rate needs T = ∞ — i.e. the input is RH itself."
