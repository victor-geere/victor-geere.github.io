## Statement

> both bound the onset only asymptotically — **n ≥ c·e^d** for J ([GOR⁺]
> Theorem 1.1, with **c never given**) …

That is the whole of the statement the source gives for this theorem; its role in
H3 is as one of the two published bounds the measured onset is compared against.

## Hypotheses and notation

- The conclusion is hyperbolicity of J^{d,n} once n ≥ c·e^d, with c a positive constant.
- **c is never given** in the source (and, the source says, in the paper as quoted here): "**c never given**".
- The source's H3 comparison: "the measured J onset grows *more slowly* than e^d over 8 ≤ d ≤ 16 — the implied c falls from 0.0171 to 0.0003 — so the exponential regime their proof needs has not begun at these degrees."

## Proof / evidence

The source states the bound without proof and without a value of c. It records that [GOR⁺]'s bound "cannot be compared at all" with the measured onset, precisely because no c is given, and that the measured J onset over 8 ≤ d ≤ 16 is slower than e^d.
