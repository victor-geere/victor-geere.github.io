# A prediction, and it was refuted (the searched optima are not scale-similar)

**Kind:** Claim
**Status:** REFUTED (prediction)
**Area:** rh-html
**Source:** [`rh27b.html`](../../html/rh27b.html) — §3 "A prediction, and it was refuted"

## Statement

> The reason one might still want a wide window is not λ but resolution: |ĥ|² is what multiplies the archimedean density, and the narrower it is in r the more the certificate is a statement about individual frequencies. At fixed shape that is exactly a scaling law — the family is scale-free, so |ĥ|²'s half-width is proportional to 1/Δ′, and the measurement confirms it to nine figures: 17.4017, 8.7008, 4.3504, ratio 0.250000. The prediction was that the searched optima would inherit it, and they do not. Their half-widths run 14.22 at Δ′ = 0.12, 6.55 at 0.24 and 31.64 at 0.48 — the last is the *broadest* of the three, where the law says it should be the narrowest by a factor of four. The searched optima are not scale-similar shapes; the search at 0.48 buys its eigenvalue with a flatter main lobe, and the resolution argument for a wide support does not survive contact with the search either. Both of rh21's directions of preference change sign once the window is searched rather than fixed.

## Hypotheses and notation

- |ĥ|² is the squared transform of the window; "half-width" is the half-width of its main lobe in r.
- At fixed shape the family is exactly scale-free, so the half-width of |ĥ|² is proportional to 1/Δ′; the fixed-shape measurement is 17.4017, 8.7008, 4.3504, ratio 0.250000 (nine figures).
- The searched optima are the twelve β-space searches at M = 16, three supports × four shapes.

## Proof / evidence

Numerical: the fixed-shape scaling is confirmed to nine figures (ratio 0.250000), and the searched optima's half-widths (14.22, 6.55, 31.64) do not follow it — the widest support returns the broadest main lobe. The source states the prediction explicitly and reports it refuted.
