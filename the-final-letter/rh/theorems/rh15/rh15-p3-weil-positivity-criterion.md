# P3 — Weil's positivity criterion (cited)

**Kind:** Theorem
**Status:** CITED (literature)
**Area:** rh-html
**Source:** [`rh15.html`](../../html/rh15.html) — §6 "What is proven, what is certified, what is not", box `P3 — Weil, cited`

## Statement

> Weil's positivity criterion: that form is nonnegative for all admissible test functions ⟺ RH. Not proven here; not in dispute.

The source states the same criterion in [rh10 §1](../rh10/rh10-riemann-weil-explicit-formula.md) as: "RH is equivalent to the right-hand side being ≥ 0 whenever *h* = |*f̂*|² — positivity of the arithmetic pairing on all test functions."

## Hypotheses and notation

- "That form" is the Weil quadratic form / explicit-formula functional of rh10 equation (1), built from primes and the Γ-factor.
- "Admissible test functions" are the compactly supported windows of the P2 box.

## Proof / evidence

The source cites the criterion (Weil 1952) and gives no proof: "Not proven here; not in dispute." The reference list of the page names A. Weil, *Sur les «formules explicites» de la théorie des nombres premiers*, Comm. Sém. Math. Univ. Lund (1952) as "the criterion cited as P3".
