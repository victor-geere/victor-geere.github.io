# THEOREM 3.10 (log-Chowla ⟺ log-Sarnak)

## Statement

> **THEOREM 3.10 (log-Chowla ⟺ log-Sarnak).** The logarithmically averaged Chowla conjecture (all `k`) and the logarithmically averaged Sarnak conjecture are **logically equivalent**, and both are equivalent to the "local Gowers uniformity" of `λ`. Also, the **un-averaged** Chowla conjecture implies the Sarnak conjecture.

The file's status line: *Status:* THEOREM. *Source:* [T. Tao, *Equivalence of the logarithmically averaged Chowla and Sarnak conjectures*, arXiv:1605.04628](https://arxiv.org/abs/1605.04628), abstract: *"Our main result is that these latter two conjectures are logically equivalent to each other, as well as to the 'local Gowers uniformity' of the Liouville function"*, and *"As is well known, this conjecture implies the conjecture of Sarnak"*.

## Hypotheses and notation

- "log-Chowla" is the logarithmically averaged Chowla conjecture for all `k` (the log-averaged analogue of [Conjecture 3.1](tpp-dict-03-chowla-conjecture.md)).
- "log-Sarnak" is the logarithmically averaged Sarnak conjecture; "local Gowers uniformity" is the property of `λ` named in the quoted abstract.
- `λ` is the Liouville function (Definition 1.2).
- The final clause relates the **un-averaged** Chowla conjecture to the (un-averaged) Sarnak conjecture.
