# exponential accuracy requires the functional equation

**Kind:** Theorem
**Status:** PROVED (paper argument)
**Area:** twin-prime-parity
**Source:** [`research/twin-prime-parity/dictionary/00-dictionary.md`](../../research/twin-prime-parity/dictionary/00-dictionary.md) — "Index. E — smoothing (`rh044/`) and the explicit formula (`rh45/`)", row E4, line 94

## Statement

The ledger row, verbatim:

> | E4 | exponential accuracy requires the **functional equation** | THEOREM | rh044 §3 |

## Hypotheses and notation

- "Exponential accuracy" is the property of a truncated/smoothed approximation to `ζ` whose error decays exponentially in `N` (the round `rh044` context of `S_N^φ`).
- "The functional equation" is the functional equation of `ζ` ([P1 of the preliminaries](tpp-dict-01-euler-product-and-continuation.md): `π^{−s/2}Γ(s/2)ζ(s) = π^{−(1−s)/2}Γ((1−s)/2)ζ(1−s)`).
