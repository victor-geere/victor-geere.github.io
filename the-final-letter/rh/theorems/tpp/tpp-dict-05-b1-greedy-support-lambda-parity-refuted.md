## Hypotheses and notation

- "The greedy support" is the set of indices taken by the greedy selection of the programme; `λ` is the Liouville function.
- `z` is the significance statistic of the parity test; the ledger quotes the nominal value `z = −47.9` and the two control values `z = +0.43` (positional control) and `z = +1.55` (rank-matched control).
- `m=2` is the repeated-position value that the ledger reports in `70.5 %` of supports and calls the source of the artefact; the dictionary does not define `m` further.
