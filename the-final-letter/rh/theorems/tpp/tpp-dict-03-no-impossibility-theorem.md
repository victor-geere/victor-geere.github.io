## Statement

> **THEOREM 5.3 (what is *not* excluded).** Nothing in the literature states that the twin prime conjecture is unprovable, or that the parity classes are inseparable by *every* method. The parity phenomenon is a limitation of a **method class**.

The file's status line: *Status:* THEOREM (trivially: no such impossibility theorem is known; the statement is recorded to prevent the parity phenomenon from being over-read as an impossibility result).

## Hypotheses and notation

- "The parity classes" are `E = {n : λ(n) = +1}` and `O = {n : λ(n) = −1}` (Definition 1.5).
- "Method class" is the class of bounded-depth sieves of Definition 1.11, to which [`THEOREM 2.1`](tpp-dict-03-selberg-parity-principle.md) applies.
