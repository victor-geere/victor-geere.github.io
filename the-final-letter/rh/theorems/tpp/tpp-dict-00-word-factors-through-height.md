## Hypotheses and notation

- `θ` parametrises the target of the greedy rule; the **height** is `sin(2πθ)`.
- "factors through" means: the greedy word is a function of the height `sin(2πθ)` alone, not of the turn count of the helix `(cos 2πθ, sin 2πθ, θ)` (see ledger row B8).
- No further hypotheses are stated in the row.
