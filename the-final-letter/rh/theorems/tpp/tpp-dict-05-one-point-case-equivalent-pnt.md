## Statement

> the one-point case `Σ_{n≤x}λ(n) = o(x)` is **equivalent to PNT** — a theorem

## Hypotheses and notation

- `λ` is the Liouville function; `Σ_{n≤x}λ(n) = o(x)` is the one-point (shift-free) case of the parity cancellation statement.
- `PNT` = the prime number theorem.
- The equivalence is stated in the ledger as a classical theorem, carrying the programme's correction label `X1`.

- The ledger records the status as **THEOREM** (classical; correction X1) and points to `03-parity-problem.md` as the source; the dictionary gives no proof and no literature citation.
