## Statement

> **OPEN 4.2.** The full `k`-point Chowla conjecture for every `k ≥ 2`; the twin-prime conjecture (Conjecture 1.8 with `h = (0,2)`); Sarnak's Möbius-disjointness conjecture; the Elliott conjecture for general bounded multiplicative functions in the un-averaged form.

## Hypotheses and notation

- The `k`-point Chowla conjecture is [Conjecture 3.1](tpp-dict-03-chowla-conjecture.md), open for every `k ≥ 2`.
- The twin-prime conjecture is [Conjecture 1.8](tpp-dict-03-hardy-littlewood-dickson-k-tuples.md) with `h = (0,2)`.
- Sarnak's Möbius-disjointness conjecture: the Möbius function is disjoint from every deterministic sequence of zero mean (the source names it without stating it separately).
- The Elliott conjecture for general bounded multiplicative functions, **un-averaged** form.

## Proof / evidence

- The source states these as **OPEN**: no proofs are known for any of the four items.
- Related known cases: `k = 1` Chowla is [THEOREM 3.3](tpp-dict-03-one-point-chowla-iff-pnt.md); the log-averaged Chowla and Sarnak conjectures are partly known ([THEOREM 3.8](tpp-dict-03-tao-log-two-point-chowla.md), [THEOREM 3.9](tpp-dict-03-tao-teravainen-log-all-orders.md)) and are equivalent to each other ([THEOREM 3.10](tpp-dict-03-log-chowla-iff-log-sarnak.md)); the un-averaged Chowla conjecture implies the Sarnak conjecture (also THEOREM 3.10).
