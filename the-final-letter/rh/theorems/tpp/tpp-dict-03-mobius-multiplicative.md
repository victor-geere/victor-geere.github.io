# THEOREM (basic) — multiplicativity of `μ` and `μ * 1 = ε`

**Kind:** Theorem
**Status:** CITED (literature; classical, no source line given in the file)
**Area:** twin-prime-parity
**Source:** [`research/twin-prime-parity/dictionary/03-parity-problem.md`](../../research/twin-prime-parity/dictionary/03-parity-problem.md) — §1.1, the "THEOREM (basic)" following Definition 1.3

## Statement

> **THEOREM (basic).** `μ` is multiplicative but **not** completely multiplicative; `μ * 1 = ε` (the unit of Dirichlet convolution), i.e. `Σ_{d|n} μ(d) = [n = 1]`; and `Σ_{n≥1} μ(n)n^{−s} = 1/ζ(s)`.

## Hypotheses and notation

- `μ(n) = (−1)^{ω(n)}` if `n` is squarefree and `μ(n) = 0` otherwise; `μ(1) = 1` (Definition 1.3), where `ω(n)` is the number of **distinct** prime factors (Definition 1.1).
- `*` is Dirichlet convolution; `ε` is its unit; `1` is the constant function `n ↦ 1`; `[n = 1]` is the indicator of `n = 1`.
- The Dirichlet series identity holds for `σ > 1`.
