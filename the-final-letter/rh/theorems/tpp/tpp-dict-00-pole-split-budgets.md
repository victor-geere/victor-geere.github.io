## Hypotheses and notation

- `P(p)`, `N(p)` are the positive and negative ζ budgets ([C1](tpp-dict-00-positive-budget.md), [C2](tpp-dict-00-negative-budget.md)).
- `p ↓ 1` means `p → 1` from above (the asymptotic regime of the pole of `ζ` at `p = 1`).
- The content is that the pole of `ζ` appears **once on each side**, each half carrying `1/(2(p−1))`.
