## Statement

> **T15** — the asymptotic sieve: a bilinear (Type-II) hypothesis yields asymptotic formulae for primes in a sieve.

The row's status and citation: THEOREM | [Friedlander–Iwaniec, *Asymptotic sieve for primes*, arXiv:math/9811186](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/9811186), Ann. of Math. (2) **148** (1998) 1041–1065 | **verified** (arXiv API: title, journal ref).

## Hypotheses and notation

- A **bilinear (Type-II) hypothesis** is a hypothesis of the form "the sequence has a bilinear decomposition whose cross-terms are equidistributed" (HEURISTIC 5.4).
- "Asymptotic formulae for primes in a sieve" means an asymptotic formula (not merely an upper bound) for the count of primes surviving the sieve.
