## Statement

> "Cassaigne gives `p(L+1)−p(L) = Σ(d⁺(f)−1)` only for an extendable language"

## Hypotheses and notation

- `d⁺(f)` is the number of right extensions of the factor `f`; the sum runs over length-`L` factors.
- "Extendable" is the hypothesis under which Cassaigne's identity is stated; the programme's word is finitely supported and eventually zero, so the language is not extendable in the relevant sense.
