1 · The brief

The arc's assessment at the close of rh11 separated the remaining goals into two lists. Not tractable, at any computational scale: proving RH (the altitude theorem of rh5 §4 — a Π⁰₁ statement can be refuted by computation, never proved by it), refuting it by search (the literature's 10¹³ verified zeros say the counterexample, if any, lives beyond reach), and constructing the base itself. Tractable, in this exact space of verified laptop computation: optimize the cone instead of sampling it; climb to a height where the statistics mature and tighter pairs live; widen to the family and watch conductor-invariance. This document executes those three, in that order, then does what an honest campaign does when its tractable list is empty: closes the books (§5) and states, precisely, what mathematical object would have to exist for the books to reopen (§6).

Part I · The three digs

2 · Dig one — the frontier: the optimal window, solved

rh11 §2 measured four window families and found the dip floor obeys the tail ordering. That left an optimization question: at a fixed gear budget — support of g inside [−2a, 2a], so only gears with log n ≤ 2a are consulted — how deep can any window drive the dip? The answer needs no search, because the scanned functional is exactly quadratic in the window. Write the profile f as a vector on nodes (piecewise-linear, even, vanishing at ±a); then h = |f̂|² makes the zeros-side reading at t₀ a quadratic form,

(1) W(t₀) = 2 ∑γ[h(γ−t₀) + h(γ+t₀)] = fT M(t₀) f,   h(0) = (b₀·f)² = 1,

with M built from the 1517 zeros plus the density tail beyond the table (so the solver cannot hide leakage past T = 2000; residual leak measured < 10⁻³ of the answer). Minimising a positive quadratic form under one linear constraint is a single linear solve: D*(a) = 1/(b₀ᵀM⁻¹b₀). One matrix, one inversion, the exact optimum over the entire infinite-dimensional family at that budget — the matched filter for the quietest point of the spectrum, tuned here to the dip at t₀* = 17.1.

Three readings. First, the answer to rh11's question in final form: at budget 12 the optimum is 170× deeper than the best fixed family reaches on 32 gears — the families were nowhere near the wall. Second, the wall itself: past ~27 gears the frontier flattens toward ~1.6×10⁻⁷, and the limiting floor is set by the zero table's own precision — the instrument is now as sharp as its spectrum is known. Third, the classical tie: minimising a positive quadratic form built from a spectrum against a bandwidth constraint is a concentration problem of Beurling–Selberg/prolate type (Vaaler's survey is the map); what the arc adds is the number: this is what the extremal theory's abstract trade-off costs, in gears, on the actual zeros of ζ. Axiom 8's instrument has been ground to its optical limit; positivity still holds at every reading it can take.

3 · Dig two — altitude: GUE arrives, and the pairs are harvested

Every table in the arc so far ended at T = 2000, because Euler–Maclaurin evaluation costs O(t) per point. The Riemann–Siegel formula costs O(√t): at t = 10⁶ the main sum is 398 terms and one correction term suffices to 3×10⁻⁶. Implemented and validated three ways — against the Euler–Maclaurin Z in the overlap, by an eight-zero splice against the rh11 table at T ≈ 2000 (ordinates matching to 10⁻³), and by the Riemann–von Mangoldt census on the new band — the machinery then harvests 60,000 consecutive zeros near t = 10⁶, scanned at step 0.05 with a change-point audit: a missed close pair leaves a permanent +2 step in the cumulative count deficit, which the audit detects and rescans at step 0.006. The final census agrees with the counting formula to within the S(T) fluctuation it cannot see (±2.5).

The hunt was pre-registered: GUE repulsion (P(s<x) ≈ (π²/9)x³ near 0) predicts the tightest of N = 60,000 gaps at a median 0.0219 of the local mean spacing. Observed: 0.0257 — inside the forecast's own spread, the third-tightest at 0.037. Each harvested pair is then collided under the backward-heat dynamics of rh11 §3, with one upgrade validated on the old table first: the frozen background is spliced — 1500 discrete neighbours a side, the density integral beyond, including the 1.7 million unseen zeros below the band — and the splice moves the validation collision by 0.02%.

The headline: λ* = −2.27×10⁻⁵, fifty-two times past rh11's −0.00119, three decades past rh10's −0.989. And with it, the hunt's complete economics, now measured rather than argued: the bound is quadratic in the rarest gap; the rarest gap among N samples shrinks like N^(−1/3); so λ* improves like N^(−2/3), and each further decade costs 10^1.5 ≈ 32× more zeros. This table cost four minutes of computation; matching Csordas–Smith–Varga's cited −4.4×10⁻⁶ needs a few hours; matching Saouter–Gourdon–Demichel's −1.1×10⁻¹¹ needs ~10¹² zeros — the literature's entire ladder is this one power law priced out. The wall at 0 (Rodgers–Tao) remains what rh11 said it was: reached by theorem, touchable from below at documented and unbounded cost, crossable only by proving RH.

4 · Dig three — the family: gears wearing characters

Nothing in the arc's machinery is special to ζ. The whole apparatus — completed function, functional equation, explicit formula, positivity criterion — exists for every Dirichlet L(s,χ), with the Generalized Riemann Hypothesis as the same knife-edge statement per conductor. Built here from Hurwitz zeta (Euler–Maclaurin, validated against three exact identities including ψ′(¼) = π² + 8G), the L-functions of the real primitive characters mod 3, 4, 5 are anchored absolutely — L(2,χ₄) equals Catalan's constant to 10⁻¹⁰, and independently the ℤ[i] lattice sum over 1.1 million points reproduces ζ(2)·G to 3×10⁻⁵, tying the construction to rh8 §6's Dedekind factorizations — and their completed Λ(½+it,χ) verified real on the line (the root number +1, Gauss's theorem, holding to 10⁻⁸).

Then the same three instruments, per conductor. Zeros: 151, 162, 170 ordinates to height 250, against counting-formula predictions of 150.6, 162.1, 170.8; first ordinates 8.039737 (χ₃) and 6.020949 (χ₄) matching the cited values to 10⁻⁵, and 6.648453 (χ₅) measured. The explicit formula: re-verified per character to 10⁻⁵ — the archimedean term shifts by log(q/π) and the parity of χ, and the prime side is the arc's same gear sum with every gear's weight rotated by χ(p): the gears wear characters. The scan: Weil positivity per character, nonnegative across the range for all three, dips to 2.5×10⁻⁵ (χ₃), 1.6×10⁻² (χ₅), 1.2×10⁻¹ (χ₄) — floor depths set by each spectrum's local gaps, exactly as for ζ.

The reading for the base question: the obstruction is uniform. Nothing about ζ's missing foundation is special to ζ — the same pairing awaits the same positivity for every character, exactly as Weil's proved case handles all characters over 𝔽q(t) at once with one surface. Whatever renders the base for ζ renders it for the family; no wall between conductors offers a side entrance.

6 · What would render a base for rh9

rh9 §8 stated the need: Weil's proof consumes a surface C × C — the curve crossed with itself over the base field — on which Frobenius has a graph, the graph an intersection number, and the Hodge index theorem a negative-definite verdict. For Spec ℤ, three things must be manufactured: a base under the arithmetic, a second factor to cross with, and a cohomology with a positive form for the crossing to act on. It is worth being precise about how close existing mathematics comes, because the answer is: one full dimension short, three live construction programs, and one clause owed by all of them.

The floor below. Arakelov geometry already supplies genuine intersection theory over ℤ: compactify Spec ℤ with its archimedean place, and arithmetic surfaces — curves fibered over Spec ℤ — carry a Hodge index theorem (Faltings, Hriljac) strong enough to prove Mordell-grade finiteness. That is the exact analogue of Weil's tool, one floor down: it treats curves over the arithmetic base, but RH needs the base itself as a curve — and then its self-product, the "arithmetic plane" Spec ℤ ×𝔽₁ Spec ℤ, an object Arakelov theory does not contain because over ℤ there is nothing beneath ℤ to cross over. Every serious construction program is a proposal for the missing tensor direction, and they differ precisely in the raw material for it:

Two of those shadows are computable from this arc's own tables, and here they are, run. The point count. If Spec ℤ were a curve over 𝔽₁, it would count points over "𝔽q" for real q > 1 by the Weil recipe N(q) = q + 1 − ∑ρqρ — the Connes–Consani program takes exactly this as the definition and inverts the Hasse–Weil formula with it. Computed from our 1517 zeros at a stated band limit, the function is not a mystery object; it decomposes, and the decomposition is the arc in one picture (differentiate von Mangoldt's formula): a smooth baseline 1 + 1/(q²−1); upward atoms exactly at the prime powers with weight Λ(n) — verified at seven gears to 5%: the points of the missing curve are the gears; negative values confined below q = 1.03, the band-limited signature of the "infinite genus" that Connes–Consani meet at q → 1; and windowed mean q + 1 to 15% — which is the prime number theorem, re-worn as "the curve has, on average, the point count of a projective line". The base's counting function exists, computes, and says nothing the primes did not already say — that is both its beauty and the program's unfinished state in one measurement.

The ledger. Deninger's proposal reads the explicit formula as a transversal Lefschetz fixed-point formula for a flow: closed orbits at the primes with period log p, the archimedean place as the fixed point at infinity, the zeros as the spectrum of the infinitesimal generator on an H¹ that does not yet exist. The formula itself, of course, exists — it is the arc's equation (1) of rh10 — and re-organized in orbit form it balances on our table to 2×10⁻⁵ (Figure 4, right). Every line of the would-be Lefschetz formula is verified arithmetic; only the cohomology it would be about is missing. That is the exact sense in which these programs are serious: they are not analogies, they are specifications — every computable consequence checks out, on this page, to stated precision.

What is owed, and by everyone. Each column above discharges the base, the plane, the Frobenius, and the trace — different raw material, same ledger — and each stalls at the same final row. Deninger needs his H¹ with a Hodge-type positivity; Connes–Consani have recast RH as a positivity of the Weil distribution on their square and proved it on subspaces (the Sonine-space results), not yet in full; Borger's Λ-geometry has the descent data but no finiteness, no H¹, no form. The arc has met this row before: it is clause (iii) of rh4 §15's Axiom 8, the one clause that was never a theorem in disguise. rh5 §5 said why the obstruction is structural (the visible symmetries are abelian; positivity of this kind is bought with the non-abelian geometry of a surface); rh9 §8 named the missing floor; §2–§4 of this document measured how sharp, how high, and how uniform the criterion holds without it. So the answer to "what alternative construction would render a base for rh9" is, in one sentence each: a cohomology of a dynamical time under Spec ℤ (Deninger), or of an adelic scale beside it (Connes–Consani), or of a Witt-vector width inside it (Borger) — any one of the three, if it can be built finite-dimensional enough to carry an intersection form, closes the arc's last axiom; and the measured state of all three is: ledger verified, positivity owed.
