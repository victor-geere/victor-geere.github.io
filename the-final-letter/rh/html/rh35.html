rh35 · the frame

The helix is the only stable geometric object the arc has: every mesh, grid and ladder is a sampling choice laid on top of it. This document carries it as an instrument rather than re-deriving it, and uses it to close rh34’s two open successors. Both close, and neither the way rh34 expected. The conditioning of the unwrapped Gram is predicted by none of the three statistics tried — the primes have the smallest rate gap of five gear sets and the highest floor — and a real search, which rh34 did not run, refutes rh32’s quadrature lemma on the unwrapped Gram as well. The lemma is now dead in both samplings.

1 · Where this stands

rh34 left three successors: (a) price the conditioning of the unwrapped Gram G[i][j] = E(log i − log j); (b) reopen rh32’s quadrature lemma on G, where rh33’s aliased counterexample cannot reach; (c) no new certificates. This document executes all three, and it does so in the construction’s own coordinates rather than in whichever grid happened to be convenient.

That change of frame is not decoration. §2 shows that the arc’s Toeplitz matrix — the object it has been bounding since rh17 — is itself the Gram matrix of a set of gear rates: sampling the moment function at v = kΔ is sampling it at the rates of ekΔ, a geometric set with irrational ratios. So rh34’s “lattice versus multiplicative” was never two objects. It was two members of one family, and the question in (a) is which member does better and why.

The answers, up front. (a) closes in its negative branch: of the minimum log-gap, the reach and the distinct-difference count, none predicts the floor, and two die by explicit counterexample. What is left is a measured threshold with no mechanism attached. (b) closes negative: six restarts of coordinate descent drive λmin(G) to a factor 0.344, past the 0.55 rh33’s aliased counterexample reached, so the lemma is refuted on the unwrapped Gram too and rh34’s reading of it was too kind by one search.

2 · The frame

The instrument is rh35_helix, and it fixes the convention once: both rotations counted the same way, so ϑ(n, t) = (n−1)π + t·log n, every increment positive in n and in t alike. rh34’s numbers regress against it — the least increment in n over n < 4000 and t out to 4800 is 3.145, the increment in t is log n — and rh3 §6’s reversal at x* = 4.4992, level 5, stays reproducible and unused, since it belongs to that document’s opposed sign convention.

It fixes the depth convention with the same hand, because the rest of this document turns on it. The gears are the levels, and they are laid out as rh3 §4 (4) lays them out: gear n at depth zn = n − 1, one unit apart the whole way down, and the unit is the first gear’s radius. Radii are rn = n−σ, so r1 = 1 for every σ: the circles taper around the ladder while the spacing stays put, and rh30 §8 detrends the chain onto exactly that axis. The gear ladder is rigidly uniform, and nothing here disturbs it.

What is not uniform is the rate. The torsion drives gear n at log n (rh3 §6 (7)), and n ↦ log n is the only thing in the construction that crowds anything: twenty-four equally spaced gears go in, and rate gaps running from 0.6931 down to 0.04256 come out. So the vocabulary is rh34 §3’s and it is kept here without slippage — a gear is a level on the unit ladder, a gear rate is its log n, and G is built on the rates, never on the gears.

Then the identity that makes it a frame. Build G on the rates of the geometric set of ratio eΔ and it reproduces the arc’s Toeplitz matrix to 4.0×10⁻¹⁵ of the mass — not approximately, but because they are the same matrix written twice. The arc has been sampling the moment function at rates the construction does not have — and it is worse than that: ekΔ is never a level, since k = log m / Δ is 5.776 at m = 2 and 9.155 at m = 3 and never an integer, so those rates belong to no gear at all.

The instrument then measures how far off. Normalise the distance from a sample to the nearest gear rate by the local gap, so that landing on a rate reads 0 and sitting as far from both neighbours as possible reads 1; uniform samples average a half, and 4,000 of them read 0.495. The arc’s sixty lattice points at Δ = 0.12 read 0.384 — closer to the construction than chance, but not by much. rh33’s six resonant moments read 0.259, and the instrument picks them out without being told which they were.

3 · What sets the floor

rh34 §6 found the multiplicative sampling worse conditioned than the lattice and blamed bunching, on one number. (a) asked for that priced. Slide the integers’ log-positions linearly towards equispaced over the same range, so that nothing changes but the spacing.

At the integers λmin(G)/mass is 0.2939. At θ = 10−4 it is 0.2938 — unchanged, so this is not a discontinuity, and the exactness of the integers’ multiplicative relations is not the mechanism. At θ = 10−2 it has doubled, to 0.589, while the positions have moved by 0.006 in log and the smallest gap has gone from 0.04256 to 0.04352. Past 5% the curve saturates, wobbling inside a factor 1.06 rather than climbing.

So the effect has a scale — about 0.006 in log, a seventh of the smallest gap — and the next question is whether any simple statistic is sensitive at it. Five gear sets at matched dimension N = 24 say no.

The primes have the smallest minimum log-gap of the five, 0.0278, and the highest floor, 0.790; the integers sit at a gap half again as wide and a floor a third the size. Correlation over the five: +0.36 — too weak to carry a story and pointing the wrong way for this one. The coincidence count fares no better: the geometric set has the fewest distinct log-differences of all, 0.08 of N², and the highest floor; the primes have the most, 0.96, and a high floor too; the integers sit in the middle at 0.62 with the lowest floor of the five (−0.15). Reach does not order it either (−0.20).

(a) therefore closes in the branch rh34 explicitly allowed for. None of the three predicts the floor; two are refuted outright. What replaces them is not an explanation but a measurement: a displacement of 0.006 in log, invisible to every statistic tried, is worth a factor two.

4 · The lemma on G

rh34 §5 tried eleven modulation frequencies by hand, found that rh33’s counterexample does not transfer, and concluded that rh32’s quadrature lemma was merely unproven on G rather than dead. It also named the failure branch: “the search drives λmin(G) below rh33’s 0.55 factor, in which case the lemma is dead on G too and (b) closes negative.”

Six restarts of coordinate descent over an eight-frequency weight profile, 7,702 evaluations, the profile held nonnegative throughout: λmin(G)/mass falls from 0.2939 to 0.1010, a factor 0.344. That is past rh34’s own 0.707 and past rh33’s 0.55. The failure branch has fired.

The optimum is degenerate, and every restart finds it to five figures: the entire budget on one frequency, log 2, at the extreme the nonnegativity constraint allows — weight 1.000 on gear 2’s rate and 2.9×10⁻¹² on everything else. Confirmed by cyclic Jacobi against Cholesky inertia bisection to 3.6×10⁻¹³.

What survives from rh34 §5 is narrower and still true: the same modulation rh33 used does not transfer, because there is no alias to carry it. A different one, built on a gear rate, does more damage than rh33’s ever did. The lemma is dead in both samplings, and the arc has no surviving version of it.

5 · The helix reading

Stated in the construction’s coordinates, the two halves of this document point at opposite ends of the same thread.

The gears are no help in locating either half, and that is the point: they are one unit apart everywhere, so the ladder distinguishes no place from any other. Both halves are located in the rates, and it is n ↦ log n that puts them where they are.

§3’s floor is set where the rates crowd. On the integers at N = 24 the closest pair is levels 23 and 24 — one unit apart on the ladder, like every other adjacent pair, but only 0.04256 apart in rate. That is the deep end, and the compression there is rh3 §6’s increment ρn+1 − ρn = −t·log(1 + 1/n) dying away: the torsion shears the deep end least, which is why rh5 §3 called the picture dull there.

§4’s attack is mounted where the rates are sparse. The frequency the search chose is log 2 — the rate of gear 2, at depth 1, the shallowest gear above the seed. Its gap of 0.6931 from log 1 = 0 is the widest in the set, at the shallow end where the torsion shears hardest. Every frequency the search kept is a gear rate and it spent nothing off them.

That is what the instrument buys. Neither statement is available in (Δ, n) coordinates, because in those coordinates neither the crowding nor the rates exist — Δ and n describe the sampling, and the sampling is not the object. Nor is either available on the ladder alone, which is uniform and therefore silent.

6 · Where this leaves the conjunction

Nowhere new, and it is worth being exact about how little was gained against how much was closed. rh32 proposed a quadrature lemma; rh33 refuted it on the wrapped lattice; rh34 showed that refutation was an aliasing artifact and reopened the question on the unwrapped Gram; rh35 refutes it there too, by a search rh34 should have run. Four documents, and the outcome is that a line of attack has been closed off from the inside.

What the frame adds is that the closing is legible. The damage lives on the gear rates and the conditioning lives in their crowding, and both are statements about the construction rather than about a grid. That is worth having and it is not worth more than it is.

The arc’s position is rh33 §7’s, unchanged: a certified-computation programme whose certified set grows only by paying for it. Nine points, and no lemma coming.

7 · What this document leaves

(a) the threshold, explained
§3 leaves a measured phenomenon with no mechanism: a displacement of 0.006 in log doubles the floor, and nothing in the gap statistics is sensitive at that scale. The obvious next instrument is the one this document did not build — the spectrum of G rather than its smallest eigenvalue, and in particular which eigenvector the perturbation destroys. If that vector is supported on the crowded deep end, the story is local and finishable; if it is spread, it is not.
(b) whether one gear rate is special or the first one is
§4’s optimum puts everything on log 2. That may be because 2 is the smallest prime, because log 2 opens the widest gap in the rate set, because gear 2 is the shallowest level above the seed, or simply because log 2 is the first nonzero rate there is — gear 1 turns at log 1 = 0, so the ladder starts with a rate the modulation cannot use. Four readings, and the search separates none of them. Re-run it on gear sets with 2 removed, and on a set whose first gear is 3, and they come apart.
(c) the certified set stays closed
Standing order, renewed a sixth time. It should be read alongside §6: with the lemma dead in both samplings, there is now no pending result that would convert measurements into certificates wholesale, and the set grows only one paid-for point at a time.
