## Statement

Let \(f(s)=\sum_{n\ge1}a_n n^{-s}\) with \((a_n)\) periodic. If \(f\) is *not* of the form \(P(s)L(s,\chi)\), with \(P\) a Dirichlet polynomial and \(\chi\) a Dirichlet character, then \(f\) has infinitely many zeros in the half-plane \(\sigma>1\) [\[23\]](../../geometric-dynamical-phase-transitions/publication.html#ref-sw). Equivalently: among Dirichlet series with periodic coefficients, non-vanishing in \(\sigma>1\) characterises exactly those that are an elementary Dirichlet-polynomial factor times a *single* Dirichlet \(L\)-function.
