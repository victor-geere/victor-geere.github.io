## Statement

For an integer \(N\ge2\), \[
\eta_N^{\mathrm{DH},(N^2)}(s)=H_{N^2}^{(s)}\bigl[a^{\mathrm{DH}}\bigr]-N^{1-s}H_{N}^{(s)}\bigl[a^{\mathrm{DH}}\bigr],
\] the pair of Definition 3.1 with the plain Euler sums replaced by their level-\(5\) twists. By Lemma C.2 it is again a combination of depth-one Euler sums, now at level \(5N\).
