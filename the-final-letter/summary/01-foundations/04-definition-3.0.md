## Statement

For real \(x\ge 0\) and a complex weight \(k\), \[
H_x^{(k)}=\sum_{n\le x}n^{-k},
\] an empty sum being \(0\). At integer weight these are the generalised harmonic numbers, the depth-one case of the finite Euler sums of the multiple-harmonic-sum literature (Hoffman [\[16\]](../../geometric-dynamical-phase-transitions/publication.html#ref-hoffman)), where the *depth* counts the nested summation indices and the *weight* the exponent. Throughout this paper the weight is the complex variable of the strip, \(k=s\); each \(H_x^{(s)}\) is then an entire function of \(s\) — a finite Dirichlet polynomial — and is the endpoint of the plain \(\lfloor x\rfloor\)-step polygon with steps \(m^{-s}\).
