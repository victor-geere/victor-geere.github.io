## Statement

For real \(R\ge 2\) and a fixed even bump \(\varphi\in C_c^\infty([-1,1])\) with \(\int\varphi=1\), \[
c_m(R)=1-R\sum_{k\in\mathbb{Z}}\varphi\bigl(m/R-k\bigr).
\] When \(R=N\) is an integer, the sharp-bump limit recovers \(c_m^{(N)}\). The associated coherent sum is \(\eta_R(X)(s)=\sum_{m\le X}c_m(R)m^{-s}\) with \(X=R^2\), and \(\rho(R,s)=\lvert\eta_R(R^2)(s)\rvert\).
