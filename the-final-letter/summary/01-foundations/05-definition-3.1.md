## Statement

For integers \(N\ge 2\) and \(M\ge 1\) set \[
\eta_N^{(M)}(s)=H_M^{(s)}-N^{1-s}H_{\lfloor M/N\rfloor}^{(s)}.
\] The coherent sum is an exact, finite combination of two depth-one Euler sums of weight \(s\); no truncation of an infinite series occurs. The quadratic link \(M=N^2\) of Section 4 makes the inner length equal to the filter period, \(\lfloor N^2/N\rfloor=N\).
