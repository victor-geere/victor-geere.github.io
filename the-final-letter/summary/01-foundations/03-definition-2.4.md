## Statement

A positive Borel measure \(\mu\) on \(\mathbb{R}\) is tempered if \[
\int_{\mathbb{R}}(1+t^2)^{-1}\,d\mu(t)<\infty.
\] The same name is used for a positive Borel measure on the truncated strip \(S_\delta\subset\mathbb{C}\) of Definition 5.0 with \(\int_{S_\delta}(1+\lvert s\rvert^2)^{-1}\,d\mu(s)<\infty\). Every tempered measure admits a unique decomposition \(\mu=\mu_{\mathrm{pp}}+\mu_{\mathrm{ac}}+\mu_{\mathrm{sc}}\) into pure-point, absolutely continuous and singular continuous parts. The residual continuous spectrum is \(\mu_{\mathrm{ac}}+\mu_{\mathrm{sc}}\).
