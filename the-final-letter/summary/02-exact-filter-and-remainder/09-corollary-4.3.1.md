## Statement

Let \(\rho=\sigma+i\gamma\) be a zero of \(\Xi\) with \(\sigma\in[\sigma_0,1-\sigma_0]\), and fix \(\varepsilon\in(0,1/4)\). Then \[
\bigl\lvert\eta_N^{(N^2)}(\rho)\bigr\rvert\ll(1+\lvert\gamma\rvert)\,N^{1-2\sigma}.
\] This is at most \(N^{1/2-\varepsilon}\) as soon as \(N^{1-2\sigma}(1+\lvert\gamma\rvert)\le N^{1/2-\varepsilon}\). In particular, every zero with \(\sigma\ge\tfrac14+\varepsilon/2\) meets the depth threshold \(N^{1/2-\varepsilon}\) for all sufficiently large \(N\) (depending on \(\gamma\)). Whether such a zero is an unrestricted closure is decided by the Marty condition of Definition 5.1 (iii): it fails on the left of \(\tfrac12-\varepsilon\) (Lemma 4.8, Theorem 5.2).

## Proof

Theorem 4.3 and \(\zeta(\rho)=0\). Compare \(N^{1-2\sigma}(1+\lvert\gamma\rvert)\) with \(N^{1/2-\varepsilon}\).
