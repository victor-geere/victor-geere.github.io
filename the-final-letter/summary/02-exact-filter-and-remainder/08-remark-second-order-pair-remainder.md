## Statement

Because the expansion is performed on each Euler sum separately, one further Bernoulli term in the classical formula (Apostol [\[12\]](../../geometric-dynamical-phase-transitions/publication.html#ref-apostol)) replaces the remainder \(O_{\sigma_0}((1+\lvert s\rvert)x^{-\sigma})\) by \(O_{\sigma_0}((1+\lvert s\rvert)^2x^{-\sigma-1})\), and hence the remainder of Theorem 4.3 by \(O_{\sigma_0}\bigl((1+\lvert s\rvert)^2N^{-2\sigma}\bigr)\) — smaller than the half-term \(\tfrac12(1-N)N^{-2s}\) by the factor \(N/(1+\lvert s\rvert)^2\), so that the half-term is an honest second asymptotic term whenever \(\lvert s\rvert=o(N^{1/2})\). For \(\lvert s\rvert\le N\) this sharpens the stated bound. Every statement below uses only the stated form.
