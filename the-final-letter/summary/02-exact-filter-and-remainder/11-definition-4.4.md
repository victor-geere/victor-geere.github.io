## Statement

On \(s=\sigma+it\) in the truncated strip of Definition 5.0, write \[
P_N(s)=\lvert 1-N^{1-s}\rvert,\qquad
F_N(s)=(1+\lvert t\rvert)\,N^{1-2\sigma},\qquad
D_N=N^{1/2-\varepsilon}.
\] These are the *main-term scale*, the *Euler–Maclaurin floor*, and the *DQPT depth*. By Theorem 4.3, \(\lvert\eta_N^{(N^2)}(s)\rvert=P_N(s)\lvert\zeta(s)\rvert+O(F_N(s))\). The three scales are of the same order (up to \(1+\lvert t\rvert\)) if and only if \(\sigma=\tfrac12\). A relative closure is \(\lvert\eta\rvert\le P_N(s)\,N^{-\varepsilon}\). A balanced closure is a relative closure that is also a slow passage in the sense of Definition 5.1: \(\lvert\eta\rvert\le D_N\), \(P_N(s)\ge N^{1/2-\varepsilon/2}\), and \(\lvert\partial_t\eta\rvert\le N^{1/2+\varepsilon}\).
