## Statement

Let \(\gamma\) be a real number with \(\zeta(1/2+i\gamma)=0\), and fix \(\varepsilon\in(0,1/4)\). Then \[
\bigl\lvert\eta_N^{(N^2)}(1/2+i\gamma)\bigr\rvert\le N^{1/2-\varepsilon}
\] for all integers \(N\ge N_0(\gamma)\), where \(N_0(\gamma)=\bigl(C(1+\lvert\gamma\rvert)\bigr)^{2/(1-2\varepsilon)}\) and \(C\) is the implied constant of Theorem 4.1. In particular \(\log N_0(\gamma)\ll\log(2+\lvert\gamma\rvert)\).

## Proof

Theorem 4.1 and \(\zeta(1/2+i\gamma)=0\) give \(\lvert\eta\rvert=O(1+\lvert\gamma\rvert)\). Compare with \(N^{1/2-\varepsilon}\).
