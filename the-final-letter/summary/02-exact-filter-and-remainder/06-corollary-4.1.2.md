## Statement

Let \(T\ge 2\) and \(N_0(T)=\bigl(C(1+T)\bigr)^{2/(1-2\varepsilon)}\), with \(C\) as in Corollary 4.1.1. Then every critical-line zero of \(\Xi\) with \(\lvert\gamma\rvert\le T\) produces a balanced path-closure for all \(N\ge N_0(T)\). The number of such zeros is \(O(T\log T)\) (Titchmarsh [\[1, §9.3\]](../../geometric-dynamical-phase-transitions/publication.html#ref-titchmarsh)).

## Proof

The map \(\gamma\mapsto N_0(\gamma)\) is increasing in \(\lvert\gamma\rvert\), so \(N_0(\gamma)\le N_0(T)\) throughout \(\lvert\gamma\rvert\le T\). Riemann–von Mangoldt supplies the count.
