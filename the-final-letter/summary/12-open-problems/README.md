# 12 — Open problems, hypotheses, and corrections

This folder is the honest ledger of what is **not** proved. It collects the remaining gap in its several equivalent forms, the one explicit arithmetic hypothesis (M), the tractable and conditional items from Addendum F, and the list of statements the paper has withdrawn or refuted.

The canonical paper proves a conversion theorem: the Riemann hypothesis is equivalent to the equality of the supports of two measure families. It does not prove that equality. Everything in this folder is an attempt to say exactly where the missing input sits, without upgrading it.

## The one-sentence summary

The unique self-dual scale $\sigma=\tfrac12$ is proved; the occupation of any other scale by a zero of Riemann’s $\Phi$ is the remaining statement. The polygon, the north-pole chart, and the last-chord product locate that statement but do not decide it.

## How to read the status language

- **Proved** — a complete argument is in the canonical source, modulo cited classical theorems.
- **Conditional** — an explicit hypothesis is stated; the conclusion follows from it.
- **Equivalent** — a reformulation of RH, not a proof.
- **Open** — no proof is known here.
- **Refuted / withdrawn** — the statement is false or the proof was invalid; the correction is recorded.
