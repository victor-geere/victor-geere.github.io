# 8. Stereographic comparison (classical, applied to \(\eta\))

> Source: [`truths.md`](../../geometric-dynamical-phase-transitions/truths.md), §8. Frozen by the canonical paper; corrections from `tensor.md` are noted in the folder README.

## Truth 8.1

If \(\lvert w\rvert\to\infty\), then \(\pi_N^{-1}(w)\) tends to the north pole. If \(w=0\), the lift is the south pole.

## Truth 8.2

Combined with 4.4: at a zero with \(\sigma<\tfrac12\) and \(\lvert\gamma\rvert\le N^{1/2-\varepsilon}\), for large \(N\), \(\lvert\eta(\rho)\rvert\ge\tfrac14 N^{1-2\sigma}\to\infty\), so the lift of the endpoint **at \(\rho\)** tends to the north pole.

## Truth 8.3

Combined with 5.2: at a local minimiser in the isolation disk of a simple zero with \(\sigma\le\tfrac12-\varepsilon\), \(\lvert\eta\rvert\) is small (so \(\eta^{\#}\sim\lvert\partial_t\eta\rvert\)) and \(\eta^{\#}>N^{1/2+\varepsilon}\) for large \(N\).

## Truth 8.4

At a simple zero with \(\sigma>\tfrac12\), the same expansion gives \(\lvert\partial_t\eta\rvert\asymp N^{1-\sigma}\lvert\zeta'(\rho)\rvert\), which is \(o(N^{1/2+\varepsilon})\) because \(1-\sigma<\tfrac12\).

## Truth 8.5

At a simple zero with \(\sigma<\tfrac12\), on the tightness window, the Marty speed **at \(\rho\) itself** (not at a nearby minimiser) is
\[
\eta^{\#}(\rho)\asymp\frac{N^{1-\sigma}}{1+N^{2-4\sigma}}\asymp N^{3\sigma-1}.
\]
The exponent \(3\sigma-1\) is negative for \(\sigma<\tfrac13\) and positive for \(\tfrac13<\sigma<\tfrac12\). This does not cancel 8.3: at a minimiser with \(\lvert\eta\rvert\) small, the denominator is \(1+o(1)\) and the speed is \(N^{1-\sigma}\).

## Truth 8.6

Near \(w=0\) the spherical metric is \(2\lvert dw\rvert\) up to \(1+O(\lvert w\rvert^2)\). Euclidean speed \(\lvert\partial_t\eta\rvert\) and spherical speed \(\eta^{\#}\) therefore coincide, up to a factor \(2+o(1)\), at any point where \(\eta\to 0\).
