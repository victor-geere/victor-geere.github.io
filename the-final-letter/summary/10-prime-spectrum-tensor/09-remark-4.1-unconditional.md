## Statement and proof

The direct route uses no information about zeros at all — only \(\Lambda(n)\) and \(\Phi\). Moreover the failure mechanism *persists under RH*: by Theorem A, concentrating \(\lvert\hat f\rvert^2\) at any real zero ordinate always produces \(\approx-\tfrac12\) per zero of the pair against a density recovery bounded by \(\sup_{\text{bump}}W<\tfrac12\). So \((\star)\) is not "the Riemann hypothesis rewritten on \(\mathbb{R}\)"; it is a statement false in every scenario.
