## Statement and proof

A zero of \(\xi\) at \(\tfrac12+\alpha+i\gamma\), \(\alpha\neq0\), is the *joint* vanishing \(K(\alpha,\gamma)=S(\alpha,\gamma)=0\). The vanishing of the cosine moment alone — 12.3's "exactly the vanishing of this moment" — is only the real half, hence necessary, not sufficient. (The programme direction survives: if \(K(\alpha,\cdot)\) never vanished for \(\alpha\ne0\) there would be no off-line zeros. But:)
