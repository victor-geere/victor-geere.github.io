# Geometric explicit formula

**Programme:** variable-radius Riemann helix / geometric reformulation.
**Source:** [`geometric-reformulation.html`](../../archive/maths/rh/research/geometrc-reformulation/geometric-reformulation.html), Conjecture 3.1; see also [`deepseek-checked3.html`](../../archive/maths/greedy-harmonic-hilbert-polya/deepseek-checked3.html).
**Status:** assumed in the archived programme; not proved. The source says its rigorous justification is equivalent to CCM Conjecture 1.

## Statement

In $\mathcal{D}'(\mathbb{R})$,

$$
\operatorname{tors}_1(t)=\tau_{\mathrm{prime}}(t)+\tau_{\mathrm{arch}}(t),
$$

where $\tau_{\mathrm{prime}}$ is the prime-power Dirac comb and $\tau_{\mathrm{arch}}$ is a smooth function originating from the Gamma factors.

## How it is used

The archived programme takes this identity as a hypothesis and derives a geometric equivalence of RH: the torsion of the variable-radius helix built from the real parts of the zeros equals the universal prime distribution if and only if the radius is constant, hence all real parts equal $\tfrac12$. The derivation is conditional on the identity.

## Status

The source is explicit: the identity is the geometric translation of the unconditional Weil explicit formula, but its rigorous justification is equivalent to the Eigencoefficient Prime Sum Conjecture of Connes–Consani–Moscovici. It is assumed for the rest of that paper. It is not a theorem, and the current canonical paper does not rely on it.
