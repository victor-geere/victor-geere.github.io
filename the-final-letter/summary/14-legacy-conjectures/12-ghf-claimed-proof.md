# GHF / proof-99 claimed proof

**Programme:** Global Harmonic Framework (GHF).
**Source:** [`paper.md`](../../archive/maths/proof-99/paper.md), Theorem 9.1 and §17.
**Status:** archived claim; the source itself notes it is unverified.

## The claim

The paper builds a greedy harmonic framework, a spectral operator $\mathcal{L}_{s,\chi}$, a determinant identity, and a Mayer–Ruelle similarity construction. Its final theorem is

> **Theorem 9.1 (Riemann Hypothesis).** Every non-trivial zero of $\zeta(s)$ lies on the critical line $\operatorname{Re}(s)=1/2$.

The route is Hilbert–Pólya: construct a self-adjoint operator whose eigenvalues are the imaginary parts of the zeros. The paper appeals to a spectral condition — that $Z'(t)/Z(t)$ is the trace of a self-adjoint operator’s resolvent — and to the simplicity of the poles.

## The source’s own caveat

The archived paper ends with:

> “The Riemann Hypothesis, as of May 2026, has not been accepted as proved by the broader mathematical community. The GHF proof presented here is the authors’ claimed resolution, pending peer review and independent verification of the Mayer–Ruelle similarity construction.”

It also records the open concern: does the proof assume all non-trivial zeros are simple (an unproven conjecture)?

## Status

Archived claim, not accepted. The canonical paper does not use the GHF construction. It is recorded here because the archive contains it and because the Hilbert–Pólya idea recurs in Addendum F as a placement, not as a proof.
