# 14 — Legacy conjectures from the archive

The repository’s `archive/` tree contains the research programmes from which the current canonical paper emerged: harmonic sine reconstruction, greedy harmonic decomposition, the correlation kernel, the Hodge-theoretic and topological approaches, the self-adjoint/CCM programme, the quantum-Egyptian functional equation, and several claimed proofs of RH. Most of it is superseded, duplicated across versions, or explicitly withdrawn.

This folder is a **deduplicated index of the explicitly labelled conjectures and hypotheses** in the major archived research programmes, together with the named results that make each one precise. It is not a reprint of the archive. Where a programme claimed a proof of RH, the claim is recorded with its archived status, not adopted.

## Why the archive is not harvested item by item

There are more than two hundred archived maths files, many of them successive versions of the same note (`v2`–`v8`, `thesis copy`, `deepseek-checked1`–`6`, duplicate `proof.html` / `ghd-proof.html`). Harvesting every file would multiply the same lemma dozens of times and would present withdrawn statements as if they were live. The canonical paper is the authority; the archive is provenance.

The selection rule here is:

1. the explicitly labelled **Conjecture** or **Hypothesis** statements of the major programmes represented in the archive;
2. the named lemma/theorem that states its equivalence or reduction;
3. one entry for each programme-level claimed proof, marked as an archived claim.

## How to read an archived claim

The archive contains sentences such as “this establishes the Riemann Hypothesis”. They are historical claims. The current canonical paper takes the opposite position: it proves a conversion theorem, shows that the additive toolbox cannot prove RH (Corollary C.10), and explicitly does not claim RH. Where an archived claim conflicts with the canonical paper, the canonical status wins. The [withdrawn-and-refuted ledger](../12-open-problems/withdrawn-and-refuted.md) is the authority.

These entries are useful for two things: they show the range of ideas that led to the canonical formulation, and several of them (CCM Conjecture 1, the torsion criterion, the topological realisation) are genuine open problems with precise statements. They are not evidence for RH.
