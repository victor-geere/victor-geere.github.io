# Dyadic sheaf cohomology conjectures

**Programme:** dyadic multiplicative sheaf cohomology on the adeles.
**Sources:** [`dyadic-sheaf-cohomology.html`](../../archive/essays/rh/dyadic-sheaf-cohomology.html); [`dyadic-sheaf-cohomology-2.html`](../../archive/essays/rh/dyadic-sheaf-cohomology-2.html), §6.3 and §7.
**Status:** explicitly conjectural; the source calls the construction speculative.

## The central conjecture

For a complex of dyadic sheaves $\mathcal{F}_0\to\mathcal{F}_1$ on $\mathbb{A}_{\mathbb{Q}}^\times$ with parameter $s$,

$$
\dim_{\mathbb{C}}H^1\!\left(\mathbb{A}_{\mathbb{Q}}^\times,\mathcal{F}_0\to\mathcal{F}_1\right)
=
\begin{cases}
0, & \operatorname{Re}s\neq\tfrac12,\\[2pt]
r, & \operatorname{Re}s=\tfrac12\text{ and }s\text{ is a non-trivial zero of multiplicity }r.
\end{cases}
$$

Equivalently, the first cohomology is acyclic off the critical line and one-dimensional per simple zero on it.

## The five central conjectures (second paper)

1. The differential $d$ is well defined after suitable regularisation, and the complex is bounded.
2. The anti-involution $\tau$ induced by $s\mapsto1-s$ satisfies $\tau^2=\mathrm{id}$ iff $\operatorname{Re}s=\tfrac12$.
3. Off the critical line the complex is acyclic ($H^1=0$), by a maximum principle on adelic height.
4. On the critical line the cohomology is exactly one-dimensional per simple zero (or multiplicity $r$).
5. The Euler characteristic (suitably defined) equals $\xi(s)$.

## Status

Conjectural. The source is explicit that the construction is speculative and far from a proof. It is not used by the canonical paper. The programme is recorded here because the archive labels the conjectures precisely, and because it is one of the clearest examples of the “geometrise RH” strategy that the canonical paper’s limitative result warns against.
