## Selection rule

1. Every statement explicitly labelled **Conjecture** or **Hypothesis** in a mathematical research page.
2. The named lemma/theorem that states its equivalence or reduction.
3. One entry for each programme-level claimed proof, marked as an archived claim.

Successive versions and duplicates are collapsed to the most informative source. The canonical paper and the [withdrawn/refuted ledger](../12-open-problems/withdrawn-and-refuted.md) take precedence over any archived claim.
