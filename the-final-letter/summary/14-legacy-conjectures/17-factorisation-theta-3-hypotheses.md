# Factorisation-theta hypotheses (part 3)

**Programme:** theta functions and divisor factorisation essays.
**Source:** [`factorisation-theta-zeta-3.html`](../../archive/essays/factorisation-theta-zeta-3.html), §§4 and 8.
**Status:** exploratory hypotheses; no precise theorem.

## Statements

- **Hypothesis (residues).** Residue methods link the integral $\int_1^\infty f(x)^{-1}x^{-s}\,dx$ to the divisor sum $\sum_{d\mid k,\ d>1}d^{-s}$.
- **Hypothesis (advanced transforms).** Advanced transforms may solidify links to $\zeta(s)$ and modular forms; the essay suggests Fourier analysis and distribution theory.

Here $f(x)=2-\cos(2\pi x)-\cos(2\pi k/x)$ has double zeros at the divisors of $k$.

## Status

Exploratory. The essay presents these as hypotheses, not results. They are not used by the canonical paper and have no role in the RH programme. Recorded for completeness because the archive labels them.
