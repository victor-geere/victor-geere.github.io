# Harmonic-sine claimed proof

**Programme:** harmonic sine reconstruction.
**Sources:** [`harmonic-sine-rh-proof.html`](../../archive/essays/harmonic-sine-rh-proof.html); [`harmonic-sine-rh-proof-v2.html`](../../archive/maths/rh/research/harmonic-sine/harmonic-sine-rh-proof-v2.html); [`harmonic-sine-rh-proof-v3.html`](../../archive/maths/rh/research/harmonic-sine/harmonic-sine-rh-proof-v3.html); gap analysis [`harmonic-sine-rh-analysis.html`](../../archive/essays/harmonic-sine-rh-analysis.html).
**Status:** archived claim; superseded by the canonical programme. Not accepted.

## The claim

The archived proof sketch reconstructs $\sin(\pi x)$ by greedy selection of harmonic angles $\pi/(n+2)$, bridges the harmonic structure to $\zeta$ through the phase function

$$
\Phi(s,\theta)=\sum_n\delta_n(\theta)(n+2)^{-s},
$$

defines a coherence defect $\mathcal{C}(\rho)$, and claims bounds using Montgomery–Vaughan, the large sieve, and van der Corput estimates. It then asserts that the functional equation forces the defect to vanish only on the critical line. The final theorem is

> **Theorem 10.1 (Riemann Hypothesis).** All non-trivial zeros of the Riemann zeta function $\zeta(s)$ lie on the critical line $\operatorname{Re}(s)=1/2$.

## Status

Archived claim, not accepted. The site itself contains a gap analysis for this programme, and the later canonical paper replaces the coherence-defect route by the exact finite Euler-sum pair and the conversion theorem. The canonical paper does not claim RH. The archived v2/v3 pages should be read as history, not as a proof.
