# p-adic / dyadic transformation conjecture

**Programme:** comparison of p-adic and dyadic sheaf cohomology.
**Source:** [`padic-dyadic-transformation.html`](../../archive/essays/rh/padic-dyadic-transformation.html), §3–5.
**Status:** conjectural functor; no proof.

## Statement

There is a functor
$$
\Phi_p:\mathrm{Sh}(\mathbb{A}^\times,\text{dyadic})\longrightarrow
\mathrm{Sh}_{\mathrm{rig}}(\mathbb{Q}_p,p\text{-adic})
$$
mapping dyadic balls to p-adic disks, with
$$
\Phi_p(\mathcal{F}_0)=\lim_k\mathcal{O}^\dagger\bigl(D(0;2^k)_p\bigr),
$$
and equating the corresponding cohomologies.

If such a functor exists, p-adic RH analogues could be transported to the dyadic setting and applied to the global RH.

## Status

Conjectural. The source says the transformation “equates cohomologies” only conjecturally; the obstacles section records why no such functor is known. It is not used by the canonical paper. Archived for provenance.
