## Statement

Let \(g\) be a test function on \(\mathbb{R}_{>0}^{\times}\) with support in \([\lambda^{-1},\lambda]\). The prime side of Weil's explicit formula, \(\sum_p\sum_{m\ge1}(\log p)\,p^{-m/2}\bigl(g(p^m)+g(p^{-m})\bigr)\), vanishes identically if and only if \(\lambda<2\). In particular the positivity of [\[35\]](../../geometric-dynamical-phase-transitions/publication.html#ref-cc2021), proved on \([2^{-1/2},2^{1/2}]\), is positivity of the explicit formula in the regime where no prime contributes; the first prime enters at \(\lambda=2\).

## Proof

Every prime power is \(\ge2\), so \(g(p^{\pm m})=0\) for all \(p,m\) exactly when \(\lambda<2\); at \(\lambda=2\) the term \(p=2,m=1\) is on the boundary of the support.
