## Excluded

- **Finance, trading, and philosophy.** `archive/ebooks`, most of `archive/essays`, and the finance portions of the root site. They contain no conjectures of this mathematical programme.
- **Visualisation code.** `complex-3d/`, `eta-term-diffs/`, `quaternions/`, `vector-calculus/`, `probability/game/`, and the Three.js/Plotly scripts. `viz.html` and `projection.html` are cited only as geometric pictures.
- **Duplicate versions.** `v2`–`v8`, `thesis copy`, `deepseek-checked1`–`6`, and duplicate filenames. The most informative source is used.
- **Raw HTML, MathJax configuration, navigation, and styling.**
- **Unlabelled speculation.** Prose that suggests a connection without a labelled conjecture/hypothesis is not promoted to one. The archive has much of this; the selection rule is label-based.

## What the summary is not

It is not a proof of RH, a replacement for the sources, or a claim that the archive is worthless. It is an index with status: it preserves the proved core, marks the conditional and open layers, and flags the contradictions so a reader does not mistake an archived claim or a withdrawn identity for a live result.
