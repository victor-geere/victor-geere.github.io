## Statement

Voronin universality [\[15\]](../../geometric-dynamical-phase-transitions/publication.html#ref-voronin) supplies discs at arbitrarily large height in \(\tfrac12<\sigma<1\) on which \(\lvert\zeta\rvert\) is below any fixed threshold with no zero of \(\Xi\) nearby, so no pointwise principle can identify closures with zeros uniformly in height. The proof above does not attempt one. Below height \((\log N)^2\) the closure threshold \(N^{-\varepsilon}\) is smaller than the classical floor of Lemma 9.4, so universality discs cannot host closures there. Above that height no identification is claimed: the anchored count of Lemma 9.2 bounds the atoms any disc can carry by the exact closures it contains, and the tempering weight integrates that to \(O(1/\log N)\).
