## Statement

A proof, had it existed here, would have been a proof that \(\int\Phi(u)\cosh(\alpha u)\cos(\gamma u)\,du\) cannot vanish for \(\alpha\neq 0\). Writing that sentence as a theorem would be lying. The adjacent truths define the object; they do not force \(\Phi\) to be in the class that kills non-real zeros. In [tensor.html](../../geometric-dynamical-phase-transitions/tensor.html) that sentence is rewritten as positive type of the completed prime current on \(\mathbb{R}\), without \(\xi\). The Jensen/Li jet remains the Cauchy picture of the same pairing ( [truths.md](../../geometric-dynamical-phase-transitions/truths.md) §15).
