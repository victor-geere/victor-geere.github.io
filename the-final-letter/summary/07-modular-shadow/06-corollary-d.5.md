## Statement

Let \(q_p(a)=(a^{p-1}-1)/p\) denote the Fermat quotient. At \(k=1\), Theorem D.4 combined with the congruences of Glaisher and Lehmer [\[20\]](../../geometric-dynamical-phase-transitions/publication.html#ref-lehmer) gives, modulo \(p\), \[
\sum_{n=1}^{p-1}c_n^{(2)}n^{-1}=2q_p(2),\qquad
\sum_{n=1}^{p-1}c_n^{(3)}n^{-1}=\tfrac32 q_p(3),
\] \[
\sum_{n=1}^{p-1}c_n^{(4)}n^{-1}=3q_p(2),\qquad
\sum_{n=1}^{p-1}c_n^{(6)}n^{-1}=2q_p(2)+\tfrac32 q_p(3).
\] Each was checked against every prime \(p<600\) not dividing \(N\), without exception (107, 107, 106 and 105 primes respectively).
