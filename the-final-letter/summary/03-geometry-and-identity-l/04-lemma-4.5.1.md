## Statement

Let \(\gamma\in\mathbb{R}\) with \(\zeta(1/2+i\gamma)=0\). Then \[
\bigl\lvert\partial_t\eta_N^{(N^2)}(1/2+i\gamma)\bigr\rvert
\le N^{1/2}\lvert\zeta'(1/2+i\gamma)\rvert+O\bigl((1+\lvert\gamma\rvert)\log N\bigr).
\] In particular, for every fixed such \(\gamma\) one has \(\lvert\partial_t\eta\rvert\le N^{1/2+\varepsilon}\) for all sufficiently large \(N\).

## Proof

Lemma 4.5 at \(\sigma=1/2\), with \(\zeta(1/2+i\gamma)=0\). The prefactor has size \(N^{1/2}\). The half-term derivative is \(O(\log N)\). Compare with \(N^{1/2+\varepsilon}\).
