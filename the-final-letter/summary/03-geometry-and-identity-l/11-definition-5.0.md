## Statement

Fix \(\delta\in(0,1/4)\). Write \[
S_\delta=\bigl\{s=\sigma+it:\delta\le\sigma\le 1-\delta\bigr\}.
\] The elementary factor \(1-N^{1-s}\) vanishes only on \(\operatorname{Re}s=1\) (Lemma 2.2), hence is bounded away from zero on \(S_\delta\): \(\lvert 1-N^{1-s}\rvert\ge N^{1-\sigma}-1\).
