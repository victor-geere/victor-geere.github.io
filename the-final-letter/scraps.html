Scraps

A harvest inventory of the research programmes that are not yet in the final letter

What this is. The final letter distils two corpora: summary/, the geometric DQPT/spectral-measure programme for the Riemann hypothesis, and summary-rh/, the greedy-harmonic-sine/twin-prime parity programme. This page is the worklist of everything else: the explicit programme folders, the standalone archive folders, the Quantum–Egyptian and formal corpora, and the essay-level veins.

It is a map, not an endorsement. Where an archived page says “this proves the Riemann Hypothesis”, the entry says archived claim; the canonical paper and the withdrawn-and-refuted ledger remain the authority. The deduplicated list of conjectures already extracted from these programmes is summary/14-legacy-conjectures/.

Reconstructed programmes. Every item in this inventory has a corresponding folder under topics/, with a dossier, a sympy computation layer and tests, and a Lean formal core. Source claims remain labelled as source claims.

How to read the status tags

6. Empty, duplicate, and container directories

  • archive/maths/rh/research/greedy-harmonic-transfer-operator/ — empty.
  • archive/maths/visualizations/ — empty.
  • archive/maths/latex/proof.tex — the LaTeX source of A Rigorous Proof of the Generalized Riemann Hypothesis via the Harmonic Sine Transform and Greedy Harmonic Decomposition (Programme IIX); a full GRH paper, companion to the HST programme. The rest of archive/maths/latex/ is its build output (proof.tex).
  • archive/maths/deepseek-checked.html and deepseek-checked-companion.html — the “Complete Greedy Harmonic Framework” summary and its companion; a loose summary of the GHF/proof-99 material (deepseek-checked.html).
  • archive/maths/correlation-kernel-research.html, hst-isometric-proof.html, and greedy-egyptian-mtcs.html — standalone papers attached to the GHD, HST, and topological-zeta programmes respectively.
  • archive/maths/plot-xi-2.html, glossary.html, and index.html — visualisation, glossary, and folder index; not programmes.
  • archive/maths/rh/2026-05-26/ — three photographs, no text.
  • archive/maths/rh/ — the container for the 17 programmes in section 2, not a programme itself.
  • archive/maths/rh/research/rh-mtc/ overlaps adelic-transfer-operator/; both use the prime-indexed MTC construction.
  • archive/qefe/ and archive/quantum-egyptian-functional-equation/ are two views of the same QEFE corpus (versions versus working drafts).
  • archive/essays/ repeats many of the archived maths papers in essay form; summary/14-legacy-conjectures/ already deduplicates their labelled conjectures.
  • archive/maths-py/ — the Streamlit numerical companions (Riemann, harmonic sine, quantum Egyptian, torsion, helix). Tooling for the programmes, not a separate programme.
  • The playgrounds (archive/complex-3d/, archive/eta-term-diffs/, archive/quaternions/, archive/vector-calculus/, archive/probability/) and the finance/architecture ebooks are visualisation or exposition projects, not mathematical research programmes.

7. Count

This inventory lists 17 explicit rh/research/ programme folders, 11 standalone programme folders/papers under archive/maths/, 3 QEFE/formal corpora, and 7 essay-level veins: 38 programme units, plus the six live/harvested items in section 1. Counts are directory-level; duplicate editions and build artefacts are not counted as separate programmes.

8. Suggested harvest order

  1. HST (archive/maths/hst and archive/maths/rh/research/harmonic-sine-transform) — the most coherent corpus and the direct upstream of the canonical paper. It has a stated programme, eight papers, and an explicit status review.
  2. GHD / correlation kernel (ghd-correlation-kernel, phase-interference, proof-99) — a claimed GRH route with exact formulas; worth auditing before the canonical paper’s Davenport–Heilbronn litmus is applied.
  3. Self-adjoint / CCM (self-adjoint-operator, geometrc-reformulation, r-helix) — contains the clearest genuinely open statement: CCM Conjecture 1 and the torsion criterion.
  4. Topological zeta / MTC (topological-zeta, rh-mtc, periodic-orbits, programme 3) — the categorical pathway; the MTC and braid-invariant machinery is the part not represented in summary/.
  5. GDST (gdst, gdst-twin-series, greedy-harmonic-zeta, theorems) — a large claimed-proof corpus with one precise equivalence and an AFP tarball.
  6. QEFE (qefe, quantum-egyptian-functional-equation, eml) — versions 1–8 already expose their own gaps; harvest the final version, not the duplicates.
  7. Adelic transfer operator (adelic-transfer-operator) — a thesis outline that overlaps the topological/MTC material; harvest only after the MTC corpus.
  8. Essay veins (quaternion–cosine, dyadic sheaf, p-adic/stochastic, sinc, factorisation–theta) — mostly speculative; summary/14 already extracts their labelled conjectures.
  9. Isabelle/AFP corpus (archive/rh-proof) — a separate formal-verification effort; treat as a corpus in its own right, not as an appendix to the paper.

Provenance and scope

The inventory was compiled from the repository’s directory tree, the programme landing pages, and the existing harvest notes in summary/00-scope-and-method/scope.md and summary-rh/00-scope-and-method/scope.md. “Programme” means a directory or paper with its own construction and a stated aim, not every HTML page. Successive versions (v2–v8, thesis copy, deepseek-checked1–6) are counted under their programme, not separately.

Links are repository-relative and resolve from the site root. Archived claims are recorded, not endorsed. The canonical authority remains publication.html and the summary status ledger.
