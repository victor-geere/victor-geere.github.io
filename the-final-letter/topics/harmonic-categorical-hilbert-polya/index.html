Harmonic–categorical Hilbert–Pólya

A reconstructed research programme: finite operator core, exact computation, archived claim

Status. The archived manuscript claims a complete Hilbert–Pólya proof of the Riemann Hypothesis. That claim is not promoted here. This folder reconstructs the finite operator/spectral algebra the manuscript writes down, proves seven small facts in Lean, and tests seven exact computations. The global proof and RH remain archived.

Inventory: Scraps item 14 · Topic index · Source: rh-proof.md (TeX, PDF).

Provenance and status

The archived manuscript is titled “A Rigorous Proof of the Riemann Hypothesis via the Harmonic–Categorical Hilbert–Pólya Operator” (V. Geere et al., dated 2026). It combines a greedy harmonic expansion of the sine function, a transfer operator on the Hardy space, finite-dimensional Hermitian truncations H_k, an eigenvalue–determinant correspondence for Δ_k(t) = det(I − A_k(t)), a Fredholm determinant identity, and a Hurwitz limit.

The archive also contains build artefacts (aux, fls, log, out, synctex, toc) and a geo/ folder. The manuscript is 195 lines; several key steps cite companion papers that are not present. The topic overlaps inventory item 17 (Harmonic Sine Transform) and item 26 (greedy-harmonic Hilbert–Pólya), and must not be treated as an independent confirmation of either.

What this reconstruction does. It extracts the finite operator/spectral algebra that the manuscript actually writes down, proving a small genuine core in Lean 4/Mathlib. It does not formalise the infinite transfer operator, the Fredholm determinant, the Hurwitz limit, or RH.

What the source claims

  • Lemma 4.1 — the eigenvalues of the Hermitian matrix H_k coincide, with multiplicity, with the real zeros of Δ_k(t) = det(I − A_k(t)).
  • Theorem 5.1 — L_s is trace class for Re s > 1 and det(I − L_s) = ζ(s)/ζ(2s) · e^{P(s)}, with a regularised extension for Re s > 1/2.
  • Proposition 6.1 — Δ_k → Δ uniformly on compact subsets of ℝ.
  • §7 / Theorem 7.1 — all zeros of Δ are real, hence RH.

Known gaps in the source argument

  1. Category mismatch in Lemma 4.1. H_k is defined at t = 0; its eigenvalues are fixed numbers, while the zeros of Δ_k(t) are parameter values. The identification needs a spectral-flow construction that the manuscript only cites (absent note [3]).
  2. Polar decomposition. A_k(t) is not assumed invertible; a polar decomposition generally gives a partial isometry, not a unitary. The continuous branch of log U_k and its differentiability are asserted, not proved.
  3. Determinant identity. Theorem 5.1 delegates the entire factor and its zero-free property to absent companion papers [6,7].
  4. Uniform convergence. Proposition 6.1 relies on estimates in absent companion paper [9]; no explicit truncation bound is given.
  5. Hurwitz passage. The argument controls limits of the real zero sets Z_k; it does not by itself exclude complex zeros of the limit Δ.
  6. Final implication. The step from real zeros of Δ to RH depends on the unproved identity (6) and the cancellation of the ζ(1+2it) pole.

The reconstruction uses a finite j-series truncation and a real transfer parameter. The structural identities are field-independent; the Python layer also evaluates the complex critical-line parameter s = 1/2 + it.

Python layer

python/programme.py is an exact sympy layer with no import side effects. It provides weight, entry, matrix_A, is_upper_triangular, diag_constant, det_A, det_one_minus_A, discriminant_2x2, charpoly_2x2, eigenvalues_2x2, and realise_symmetric_2x2. All arithmetic is exact rational/algebraic; the critical-line check uses the exact complex parameter s = 1/2 + i/3. mpmath is not needed.

cd the-final-letter/topics/harmonic-categorical-hilbert-polya
python3 -m pytest -q tests

Recorded result: 7 passed (about 10 s).

Lean layer

lean/Topic.lean imports Mathlib, opens namespace HCHP, and proves T1–T7. It uses a real transfer parameter and a finite j-series truncation; the complex analytic statements of the source are outside its scope.

cd the-final-letter/topics/harmonic-categorical-hilbert-polya
./lean/check.sh

The script delegates to ../tools/check-lean.sh, using the local Mathlib at ~/lean/mathlib4 (Lean v4.35.0-rc2). Recorded build: lean/BUILD.log.

Not formalised: L_s, trace-class/Fredholm theory, polar decomposition and logarithm, H_k, uniform convergence, Hurwitz’s theorem, and RH. See lean/README.md.
