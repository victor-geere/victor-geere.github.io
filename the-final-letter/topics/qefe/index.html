Quantum–Egyptian functional equation (QEFE)

Status: archived claim reconstructed as an elementary formal core plus an exact computation layer. The central functional equation is not promoted to a theorem.
Source: archive/qefe/quantum-egyptian-functional-equation-v8.html (v8, March 2026; v1–v7, TeX source, and earlier drafts in the same folder).
Scraps · Topic index · The Final Letter

Honesty summary. The source's Lemma 2.2 (the Egyptian-fraction identity) is correct and is fully formalised here. The central “Theorem 3.1” is an archived claim: its proof is explicitly interrupted, its Mellin normalisation changes between sections, and the source's own Ising and SU(2)₄ data satisfy the displayed identity only at s = 1/2. The Riemann-hypothesis material in §9 is speculative and is not a result of this reconstruction. Nothing here implies RH, GRH, twin primes, Chowla, Sarnak, or Elliott.

Provenance and what the source contains

The QEFE corpus is a single-author programme, not a published result. Version 8 is the latest archived draft and presents itself as “complete, rigorous, and self-contained”, with “two distinct proofs (lattice-theoretic and conformal-character)”. The text actually contains one proof sketch: a lattice realisation of an integral modular tensor category (MTC), a Poisson-summation argument for a twisted theta function, and a Mellin-transform derivation of a Riemann-zeta-type functional equation. The two proofs promised in the introduction are not both present.

The source defines:

  • the Egyptian denominators mᵢ = D² / dᵢ² of an integral MTC, where D² = Σᵢ dᵢ² is the squared total quantum dimension;
  • a Galois character χ(mᵢ) = εᵢ(σ) read off the action of a Galois automorphism σ on the modular S-matrix;
  • the categorical Dirichlet series Ψ(s) = Σᵢ χ(mᵢ) mᵢ−s;
  • a twisted theta function Θ(τ) = Σᵢ χ(mᵢ) exp(−π (mᵢ/D)² τ) and its Mellin transform;
  • a claimed functional equation of Riemann-zeta type, with parity a ∈ {0,1}, root number ε = ±1, and a Galois-conjugate category;
  • explicit data for the Ising MTC and SU(2)₄;
  • speculative sections on Chern–Simons theory, the Langlands programme, a limit to ζ(2s), and the Riemann hypothesis.

The archived status of the central claim is source claim / unproved. The Riemann-hypothesis material is speculative and is not a result of this reconstruction.

Reconstruction strategy

The source has a genuine, fully elementary core: the Egyptian-denominator algebra and Lemma 2.2. That core is formalised in Lean and checked exactly in Python. The analytic functional equation, the lattice theta transformation, and the Galois sign identity are kept as source claims. The Python layer also tests the source's own examples against its displayed equation, which is how the off-centre failure is documented rather than hidden.

The distinction matters: T1–T7 are elementary facts about the objects the source defines. C1–C8 are exact or high-precision calculations. S1–S4 and O1–O2 are recorded with their archived or open status and are not theorems of this reconstruction. Neither the Theorem nor the Computation rows contain Theorem 3.1 of the source.

Why the central claim is not a theorem here

  1. The derivation is explicitly interrupted. In §5.1 the text derives an identity, observes that it “merely reproduces the original first part”, and then says “Instead, we obtain the functional equation … after a different manipulation”. That different manipulation is never supplied.
  2. The Mellin normalisation is inconsistent. §2.4 computes the Mellin transform with a factor D2s and Γ(s); §5.3 replaces it with Ds and Γ((s+a)/2) by hand, invoking a “standard result” about theta characteristics without matching it to the categorical theta series. The parity a is asserted, not derived.
  3. The worked examples do not satisfy the displayed equation off-centre. Using the tabulated data and the source's own choices ε = 1, a = 0 (Ising) and a = 1 (SU(2)₄), the exact residual Ψ(s) − RHS(s) is:

  4. The root number is internally inconsistent. Appendix A defines ε = ∏μ εμ / |∏μ εμ|. For Ising the signs are (1, −1, 1), so the product is −1, while §6.1 states ε = 1. C7 records this.
None of this shows that no corrected categorical functional equation exists. It shows that the v8 statement, as written, is an archived claim with a broken proof and counterexamples among its own examples.

Open items and known gaps

  1. Correct formulation. Determine the correct Mellin normalisation, parity convention, and root-number definition for a categorical Dirichlet series. Until then the QEFE is a conjecture-shaped source claim.
  2. Theta modularity. Formalise the lattice realisation and the Poisson summation step (Lemma 4.2), including the rank and determinant hypotheses.
  3. Galois sign identity. Prove the Appendix A identity from the de Boer–Goeree / Coste–Gannon / Bantay equivariance instead of citing it.
  4. Root number. Reconcile the product-of-signs definition with the per-example values; the Ising case is already inconsistent.
  5. SU(2)k limit. State and prove a limit theorem for the normalised series, or record precisely why the Galois signs obstruct it.
  6. Scope of T5. T5 assumes a finite list of positive dᵢ² summing to D² with d₀² = 1. It does not construct an MTC or verify integrality from categorical axioms; that input is taken as a hypothesis.

Python layer

python/programme.py provides:

  • egyptian_denominators(D2, dsq) — exact mᵢ, with validation;
  • egyptian_reciprocal_sums(D2, dsq) — total and non-unit Egyptian sums;
  • ising() and su2_4() — the two worked examples, with signs and parity;
  • categorical_psi(m, chi, s) and psi_of(data, s) — exact Dirichlet series;
  • gamma_ratio(a, s) and qefe_prefactor(D2, a, s) — gamma/prefactor data;
  • qefe_rhs(data, s, epsilon=None) and functional_equation_residual(...);
  • sign_product(data) — the Appendix A root number from the signs.

Run the tests from the topic folder:

cd the-final-letter/topics/qefe
python3 -m pytest -q tests

Current result: 8 passed in under two seconds. Only sympy is required; mpmath is not needed because all checks are exact or evaluated by sympy.N.

Lean layer

lean/Topic.lean imports Mathlib, opens the QEFE namespace, and proves T1–T7. It defines egyptianDenom, egyptianUnit, gammaRatio, and qefePrefactor. There is no sorry, axiom, or admit. 

cd the-final-letter/topics/qefe
./lean/check.sh

The wrapper calls the shared tools/check-lean.sh against the local Mathlib. Recorded build: lean/BUILD.log — [check] OK Topic.lean. What is and is not formalised is set out in lean/README.md.
