Geometric reformulation

A reconstructed research programme: helix/torsion formal core, exact computation, source claims

Status. Elementary formal core and exact computation layer reconstructed. The geometric torsion identity (Conjecture 3.1), the conditional equivalence with RH (Theorem 4.1), and the CCM dictionary remain source claims / open. The full ledger, provenance, and corrections are in README.md.

Source: archived paper A Geometric Framework for the Riemann Hypothesis: Twin Helices, the Riemann Helix, and the CCM Spectral Triple. Inventory: Scraps item 10. Topic index: topics/.

What the programme is

The archived paper proposes a geometric restatement of the Riemann Hypothesis in four layers.

1. Twin conical helices

For s = σ + it with σ > 1, the Dirichlet terms n−s and n−s̄ are embedded at height n:

C±(x) = (x^(−σ) cos(t log x), ± x^(−σ) sin(t log x), x),  x ≥ 1.

Both curves lie on the cone r = z−σ and wind in opposite directions. They intersect when sin(t log x) = 0, i.e. at xk = exp(kπ/t), giving the planar polygon points

P_k = ((−1)^k x_k^(−σ), 0, x_k).

2. Geometric prime distribution

The paper applies Möbius inversion to a superposition of the twin helices and asserts the distributional measure

τ_prime(t) = Σ_(p,m) (log p / p^(m/2)) δ(t − m log p).

3. Riemann helix and variable radius

Given zeros ρ_n = β_n + iγ_n with 0 < γ_1 < γ_2 < …, choose an increasing phase φ with φ(γ_n) = 2πn, and set

C_1(t) = (cos φ(t), sin φ(t), t),
C_r(t) = (r(t) cos φ(t), r(t) sin φ(t), t),   r(γ_n) = β_n.

The paper states the Geometric Explicit Formula (Conjecture 3.1), tors_1 = τ_prime + τ_arch, and claims the conditional Theorem 4.1: assuming Conjecture 3.1, RH is equivalent to the existence of a smooth r with r(γ_n) = β_n and tors_r = tors_1.

4. CCM dictionary

The paper identifies the picture with the Connes–Consani–Moscovici spectral triple: a Dirac operator with point interactions at prime logarithms, a finite Fourier truncation Q_(kℓ), and the Eigencoefficient Prime Sum Conjecture as the quantum counterpart of Conjecture 3.1.

Reconstruction boundary. This folder reconstructs the finite, checkable geometry: torsion of constant-pitch helices, the conical radius and intersection polygon, prime-power weights, and a finite Fourier matrix modelled on the source's Q_(kℓ). RH and CCM Conjecture 1 are not proved here.

See lean/README.md for the precise boundary.

Open items and known gaps

  1. Conjecture 3.1 (Geometric Explicit Formula). Give a rigorous distributional proof, or a precise counterexample. This is the source's main open item.
  2. Repair Theorem 4.1. The constant-radius step is false (T5) and the density claim for {m log p} is unsupported. As printed, the theorem does not establish RH.
  3. Correct the torsion formula (3.2). Replace the archived denominator by φ′⁶+φ′⁴+φ″² and formalise the non-linear-phase case.
  4. Correct the Möbius identity. Use Σ_n (μ(n)/n) log ζ(ns) = Σ_p p−s, then justify the further step to τ_prime.
  5. CCM layer. Construct the self-adjoint Dirac operator with point interactions, identify the finite truncation, and state the Eigencoefficient Prime Sum Conjecture precisely.
  6. Explicit formula. Relate the torsion distribution to the Weil explicit formula; the source asserts this but does not prove it.

The reconstruction has a genuine elementary core (torsion bounds, cone-radius monotonicity, prime-weight positivity, finite Hermitian matrix) and a large speculative periphery. No archived RH, GRH, twin-prime, Chowla, or CCM claim is promoted to a theorem.
