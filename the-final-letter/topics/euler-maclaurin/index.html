Focused annex to Objective III of The Final Letter.

Status: structural pass. This dossier documents and mirrors the Euler–Maclaurin development that turns the two former source claims S1–S2 into Lean theorems for K ≥ 1. The authoritative full dossier remains topics/partial-sum-plus-tail.

What this layer is

For a > 0, s = σ + it with σ > 1, M ≥ 1, b = a + M, rising factorial (s)n = s(s+1)…(s+n−1) and Bernoulli numbers B2j, the non-periodic Hurwitz zeta satisfies

ζ(s,a) = Σk<M(a+k)−s + ∫M∞(a+x)−sdx + b−s/2 + Σj=1K B2j/(2j)! · (s)2j−1 · b−s−2j+1 + RK(s),

with the explicit tail bound

|RK(s)| ≤ |(s)2K+1| · 2ζ(2K+1)/(2π)2K+1 · b−σ−2K/(σ+2K).

The Lean development proves the finite Euler–Maclaurin identity, the Fourier inversion and Bernoulli supremum bound that control the periodic Bernoulli functions, the differentiability and iterated derivatives of the Hurwitz term, the integral form of the tail bound, and the two displayed statements for K ≥ 1.

python/programme.py uses sympy for exact rational work and mpmath for the tail illustration. It is importable without side effects. K ≥ 1 is enforced for tail_radius because that is the hypothesis of the Lean S1/S2 theorem; K = 0 is allowed for the finite identity, which Lean proves for all K.

What is not formalised

  • The lowering of the general S1/S2 theorem into the evaluator's certificate Replay for arbitrary s, a and Dirichlet character (R13). This is engineering, not new analysis. The target interface, the W1–W5 decomposition and the acceptance criteria are specified in notes/replay-wiring.md.
  • A certified enclosure width. inflate is sound for every non-negative radius, so the recorded narrow widths are measurements, not theorems (R15).
  • Use of a certified enclosure as a hypothesis in a theorem about a wall (R14). Nothing in this folder changes the wall map.
  • The s = 1 pole and L'/L derivatives, which belong to the larger evaluator dossier rather than to the Euler–Maclaurin identity itself.

Relation to the walls

This layer is the analytic core of the third objective, not a bridge. It makes the evaluator's decomposition unconditional. It does not prove RH or the twin-prime conjecture, and no theorem about either wall consumes the enclosure. The bridge requirements remain global positivity on the Riemann side and a positive-density multiplicative coupling on the parity side; see status.html §§5–6 and 11 — Bridges.
