EML universal operator

A fragment reconstructed as a small formal core, an exact computation layer, and archived source claims

Status. Fragment reconstructed. The elementary eml identities are proved in Lean and tested exactly in Python. The archived zeta and Riemann-hypothesis material is preserved as source claim and conjecture, never as a theorem. Full provenance and the same ledger are in README.md.

What the programme is

The source is a short generated conversation record (15 April 2026) with two TeX write-ups. Its mathematical substance is the binary operator

eml(x, y) = exp(x) − log(y),    y > 0,

with the constant 1, and the base identities

exp(z) = eml(z, 1)
log(z) = eml(1, eml(eml(1, z), 1))
sqrt(u) = eml(log(u)/2, 1)

The source then asserts functional completeness for elementary functions, recasts one step of Geere's greedy harmonic sine reconstruction as a finite eml tree, and sketches a link to the Riemann zeta functional equation followed by a conjectural route to RH.

This folder reconstructs only the elementary, checkable part: the operator, the two base identities, the square-root identity, and the order structure of eml · 1. The three-gate logarithm identity is the nontrivial formal result. Functional completeness, the harmonic fusion rule, the correlation kernel, and the RH sketch are recorded with their source status.

A source-level defect worth recording. The printed fusion rule is

s(n+1) = s(n)·sqrt(1−h(n)²) + δ(n)·( h(n)·sqrt(1−s(n)²) + s(n)·sqrt(1−h(n)²) )

For δ(n) = 1 this is 2·s·cos h + h·cos s, not the sine-addition value s·cos h + h·cos s. The Python listing uses a third variant, s += h·sqrt(1−s²) + s·sqrt(1−h²). Both readings fail once two harmonic angles are selected. At θ = 5π/12 the printed recurrence gives about 0.567 and the listing about 1.83, against sin(5π/12) ≈ 0.966. The corrected recurrence used here is ordinary sine addition applied only on a selection. This is a defect in the source, not a result of the reconstruction; see computation C5.

Source assessment

  • Fragment, not a programme. The HTML is a 13 KB conversation transcript. The two TeX files eml.tex and eml-harmonic-zeta.tex are the write-ups; the HTML files are renderings.
  • Genuine elementary core. The two base identities and the square-root identity are correct; eml.tex gives the algebraic verification, and this folder formalises all three in Lean.
  • Functional completeness is borrowed. It is attributed to Odrzywołek (March 2026) and is not proved in the source. No eml-tree syntax is formalised here.
  • The fusion rule is defective as printed. The prose recurrence, the displayed geometric rule, and the Python listing are not the same map, and none is sine addition. The corrected recurrence is recorded and tested, not silently substituted.
  • The zeta/RH material is archived. The functional equation is quoted without proof, the bridge is motivational, and the RH conjecture is explicitly conjectural. Nothing here upgrades it.

Python layer

python/programme.py implements eml, exp_via_eml, log_via_eml, sqrt_via_eml, the two source fusion readings, the corrected harmonic_sine, and selection_count. The identity checks are exact sympy simplifications; the greedy loops use floating point and state their tolerances in the tests.

cd the-final-letter/topics/eml-universal-operator
python3 -m pytest -q tests

Open items and gaps

  1. eml-tree syntax and completeness (O1). Universality needs an inductive syntax, evaluation semantics, and closure proofs. Only the base identities are formalised here.
  2. The fusion rule (O2, C5). The source recurrence must be repaired before its O(1/N) theorem can be assessed. The corrected recurrence is tested on [0, π/2]; the full interval and the stated bound remain open.
  3. Correlation kernel (O3, S4–S6). The diagonal formula and spectral bounds are plausible for a corrected threshold model but are not derived or formalised here.
  4. The zeta bridge and RH (S8–S9, O5). The archived zeta/RH material is motivational. The Riemann hypothesis, GRH, twin primes, Chowla, and related statements are not proved, assumed, or used as axioms anywhere in this reconstruction.
  5. Provenance of the generated text. The conversation record is not peer-reviewed; claims should be checked against the two TeX write-ups and against Geere's greedy-harmonic programme before reuse.
