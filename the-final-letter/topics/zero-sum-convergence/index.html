Convergence of the zero sum

Status: the trivial zeros are infinite, the Gaussian series over them is summable and has a HasSum, and ζ has no zeros on Re s > 1. The nontrivial-zero counting bound is recorded as the named input ZeroCountingBound; the reduction to the summability of the zero sum is open. Nothing here proves the Riemann hypothesis.

This is direction 2 of the two repairs identified in archimedean-prime-evaluation as O4. See also zeta-functional-equation (direction 1, O3).
