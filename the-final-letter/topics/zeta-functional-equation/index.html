The zeta functional equation and the continuation of ζ′/ζ

Status: the functional equation, the analytic continuation of ζ away from 1, the zero correspondence between ζ and Λ, and the analyticity of ζ′/ζ away from the zeros are proved. Nothing here proves the Riemann hypothesis.

This is direction 1 of the two repairs identified in archimedean-prime-evaluation as O3: the explicit formula's prime side needs ζ′/ζ on the critical line, which needs the functional equation and the continuation. See also zero-sum-convergence (direction 2, O4).

Provenance

Everything is proved from Mathlib's Riemann-zeta development: completedRiemannZeta_one_sub, riemannZeta_one_sub, differentiableAt_riemannZeta, riemannZeta_def_of_ne_zero, riemannZeta_neg_two_mul_nat_add_one. The work here is the organisation into the statements the explicit formula needs, plus the logarithmic-derivative results, which Mathlib does not state.

What this does and does not give

The circle case of the residue theorem is proved (T34–T36, D3). The rectangle statement is formalised (D1/D2, T33) but its proof needs O3b (rectangle-minus-discs Cauchy) and O3c (removable singularity).

The analytic prerequisites for O3 are proved: continuation, ζ′/ζ on the critical line, left half-plane zeros, the residue at simple zeros, the residue decomposition, and the non-summability of Σ Λ(n)/√n. O3a is now written in Lean as RectangleResidueTheorem / ContourShiftStatement, and T33 reduces it to the vertical-integral difference. Only the proof of the residue theorem is missing, because Mathlib has no argument principle for meromorphic functions. No Riemann-hypothesis claim, no assertion of simplicity of all zeros.
