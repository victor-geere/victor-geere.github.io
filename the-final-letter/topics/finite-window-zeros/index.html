Finite-window zeros

A P3 dossier of The Final Letter.

Status: P3 has a point and a thin segment. The finite algebra, the pair estimate, the identity (1-2^{1-s})ζ(s) = η(s) for every 0 < Re s, s ≠ 1, the tail bound and the verified complex-power atom are Lean-proved. Cert.lean assembles a 100-pair certificate and proves ζ(1/2 + 5i) ≠ 0 with no atom hypothesis. This is a pointwise finite-window theorem, not a zero count and not a statement about RH; a segment remains open.

Provenance

P3/R14 of status.html §7. The eta/zeta identity is the standard alternating-series identity; the programme proves it for Re s > 1 in SourceClaims.lean. Inventoried as item 47 in scraps.html.

What the dossier is

P3 asks for one theorem whose numerical premise is discharged by a Lean certificate. The chosen finite-window object is a segment of the critical line, reached through the grouped alternating (eta) series

η(s) = Σ_{n≥1} (-1)^{n-1} n^{-s}
     = Σ_{m≥0} ((2m+1)^{-s} - (2m+2)^{-s}).

The pairing is absolutely convergent for Re s > 0, and the classical identity

(1 - 2^{1-s}) ζ(s) = η(s)        (E)

holds for Re s > 0. A certified rectangle for η plus a certified lower bound for |1 - 2^{1-s}| encloses ζ on the same window; a box that excludes zero certifies nonvanishing there. This is a finite-window statement. It does not assert RH and cannot decide an off-window zero.
