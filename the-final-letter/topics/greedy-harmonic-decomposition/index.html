Greedy harmonic decomposition

A reconstructed research programme: formal core, exact computation, source claims

Status. Formal core and computation layer reconstructed. Source claims are preserved separately. The full reconstruction, theorem ledger, and provenance are in README.md.

What the programme is

The greedy harmonic decomposition writes a target angle θ ∈ [0, π] as a sub-sum of the harmonic angles α_n = π/(n+2). At step n it selects α_n when the residual is at least α_n; the resulting binary word reconstructs sin θ. The threshold paper proves θ_n* = α_n and gives closed forms for the correlation kernel K(n,m).
