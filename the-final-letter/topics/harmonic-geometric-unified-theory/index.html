Harmonic–Geometric Unified Theory plan

A seven-phase research plan, reconstructed as a minimal exact core and a ledger of open claims

Status: plan only. This page reconstructs the programme's elementary harmonic/Egyptian arithmetic and records its analytic claims as source claims or open items. It does not claim the Harmonic–Geometric Unified Theory, the Spectral Riemann Hypothesis, or the Generalised Riemann Hypothesis as proved. The full dossier is in README.md.

What the source is

The source, archive/maths/research-programme.html (May 2026), is a three-year, seven-phase research plan. Its overall goal is to “derive the Riemann zeta function and its generalizations from the greedy harmonic expansion of angles, and prove the Spectral Riemann Hypothesis together with its generalizations”.

The plan names the Greedy Harmonic Tree, the Harmonic Sine Transform, the transfer operator L_s, Greedy–Egyptian Modular Tensor Categories, and the Quantum–Egyptian Functional Equations, but supplies no definitions, lemmas, or proofs. It does not define the “greedy harmonic expansion of angles” or the “Egyptian” construction beyond their names. The companion topic Greedy harmonic decomposition reconstructs the angle expansion; this folder does not duplicate it.

Open items and known gaps

  1. No analytic definitions in the source. L_s, the Fredholm determinant, P(s), the twisted operator, and the MTC data are named but never defined.
  2. Spectral equivalence and Spectral RH. det = 0 ↔ ζ(1/2+it) = 0 is the central target and is entirely open.
  3. Monotonicity inequality. The Phase 4 inequality is stated without proof; GRH is conditional on it.
  4. Zero-free factor. The source itself flags eP(s) zero-freeness as the weakest link.
  5. Greedy–Egyptian categories and QEFE. No construction or analytic continuation is supplied here.
  6. Schedule inconsistency. The phase maxima total 108 months, while the milestones propose a 36-month horizon; the plan only works with heavy overlap. This is a planning observation (C6), not a mathematical result.
  7. The harmonic/Egyptian core is reconstructed, not quoted. The source does not define w n, α n, or the greedy Egyptian algorithm. The formal core is the elementary arithmetic those names presuppose, not the theory's analytic content.
