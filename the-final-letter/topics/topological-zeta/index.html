Topological zeta

A reconstructed research programme: finite MTC formal core, exact computation, source claims left open

Status. Reconstructed finite formal core and exact computation layer. The RH/topological realisation is retained as a source claim / open, never as a theorem. The full dossier, provenance, and theorem ledger are in README.md.

Source. archive/maths/rh/research/topological-zeta/index.html (a link hub for eleven pages).
Inventory. Scraps item 23 · Topic index. The inventory records an overlap with item 21.

1. What the programme claims

The corpus is a unified narrative from “geometric sine” to a would-be proof of the Riemann Hypothesis: a trigonometric-free sine construction by dyadic bisection and greedy harmonic selection; Egyptian-fraction decompositions; a “quantum Egyptian” functional equation attached to modular tensor categories (MTCs); greedy selectors identified with Galois characters to form Greedy-Egyptian MTCs; the zeta function realised as a limit of Reshetikhin–Turaev braid invariants, with the critical line as a symmetry axis and zeros as defects; and a numerical “non-vanishing floor” programme.

The source is explicit in several places that the later stages are frontier, conditional, or speculative. The introduction says “No published proof exists—this is frontier conjecture”; the proof sketch labels its inputs Conjecture 1 and an asymmetry lemma; the numerical papers say numerics “does not replace human proof”. The part of the corpus that is genuinely finite and checkable is the modular data algebra: quantum dimensions, the total dimension, Egyptian denominators, the Ising/SU(2)₄ S-matrices, and the finite Dirichlet polynomials.

2. Provenance

The archived folder contains twelve files (the index plus eleven pages). The most careful is mtc-dirichlet-functional-equation.html, which states finite-rank MTC definitions, the Egyptian identity, the Dirichlet polynomial, a conditional functional equation, explicit Ising and SU(2)₄ examples, and three open problems. The introduction is a speculative ebook whose chapter 5 claims RH “in this speculative framework” and whose chapter 6 retracts it as not a formal proof. The proof sketch states the conditional “Topological Zeta Rigidity” theorem. The Greedy-Egyptian MTCs paper identifies greedy selectors with Galois characters and contains the Egyptian-identity misstatement corrected below. The numerical and rigorous-proof papers describe the floor/error crossing and the four analytic steps still needed. The unified paper, limiting-factor paper, and the quaternion-lifting papers complete the corpus. It is coherent but repetitive, and is not a proof of RH.

The “topological zeta” of the title is not a new zeta function defined in the corpus: it is the name for the proposed limit lim_k Z_k(s) = ζ(s) of normalised Reshetikhin–Turaev invariants. That limit is a conjecture.

7. Errata and limitations

  1. The stated functional equation is false for the stated completion. With q = 4, a = 0, Ψ(s) = 2·4^{−s} − 2^{−s}, the ratio Λ(s)/Λ(1−s) takes values approximately −0.63 − 0.15i, 0.043 + 0.20i, and −0.0038 − 0.019i at s = 0.7+2i, 1.5+3i, 2.0+i. No constant ε can satisfy the claimed equation. The source's “10 significant figures” verification is not reproducible.
  2. The Egyptian identity is misstated in the greedy paper. The full sum is 1, not 1 − 1/D²; the latter is the sum over non-unit objects. The later mtc-dirichlet-functional-equation.html paper contains the correction.
  3. “Topological zeta” is a proposed limit, not a defined object. The corpus does not define the braid family or the RT invariant precisely enough to state Conjecture 1 as a mathematical conjecture in a fixed formalism.
  4. The RH conclusion is conditional and speculative. The source's own caveats (intro chapter 6; proof-sketch conclusion; numerical paper conclusion) say so. This dossier follows those caveats.
  5. The quaternion-lifting papers are not reconstructed here. They are a separate reformulation of the “geometric RH”; they are listed in provenance but are not part of the finite MTC core.
