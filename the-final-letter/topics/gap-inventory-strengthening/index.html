What the source is

gaps.html is a sidecar to the merged paper. It is a status file, not a paper:

  • a table of 21 closed-item rows (some rows group several labels) (A, B, C, D, G, …, P8, L₀, T) with the lemma or theorem said to close each;
  • a table of "closed identities" (L, Lm, Lband, T, DH, D, E, F) recording what the source says was proved;
  • a table of "tractable items" from Addendum F, with statuses proved, conditional, tractable, not done, and open;
  • a prose section, "The remaining gap, collapsed", which isolates the final missing statement.

The body of gaps.html contains no complete proofs. Every status word in it ("proved", "unconditional", "sharp") is an archived source claim in the sense of the programme standard. This reconstruction does not re-verify the paper. It takes the one place where the source writes an exact, self-contained identity — the last-chord product — and builds a small Lean/Python core around it. Everything else is recorded below as a source claim or an open item.

The scraps description also mentions "the refined quadratic asymptotic of Addendum A". That asymptotic is a separate archived claim in publication-addendum-A.html (a stationary-phase expansion with exponent δ = 29/168 − η); it is not developed in gaps.html, has no complete proof in the source, and is recorded here only as a source claim.

The precise identity that anchors the formal core

In "The remaining gap, collapsed", the source writes the last chord and its product across s and 1 − s:

HN(s) = ((1 − N)/2) · N−2s,    HN(s) HN(1 − s) = (N − 1)² / (4N²) → 1/4.

The product is an identity of monomials and is independent of s. At a displacement s = 1/2 + δ the two factors separate as

HN(1/2 + δ) = CN N−2δ,    HN(1/2 − δ) = CN N2δ,    CN = (1 − N)/(2N),

so one factor tends to 0 and the other to ∞ as N → ∞, while their product stays at (N − 1)²/(4N²). That is the entire algebraic content of the source's off-line dichotomy. It is not a statement about zeros of ζ: the source itself says "Both factors remain of order one if and only if σ = 1/2", and that conditional reading depends on the unproved tracking estimate. The formal core stops at the monomial identity.

Open items and gaps

  1. The remaining slice. For K(α,γ) = ∫0∞ Φ(u) cosh(αu) cos(γu) du, the source reduces the gap to: for α ≠ 0 and γ ≠ 0, K(α,·) vanishes only if it changes sign. This is open in the source; no Lean statement is made about it.
  2. F.4 — Lee–Yang confinement for ηN(N²). An interacting ferromagnetic representation whose finite partition functions are the polygon. Open in the source.
  3. Item (b), the transfer-operator form of F.4. Highest expected return according to the source, but a finite computation not carried out.
  4. Items (a), (c), (d). Gonek–Ledoan for ηN, the heat-flowed polygon, and the twisted F.8 are all "tractable, not done" in the source.
  5. Conditional uniformity. Uniform slow passage for ε ≤ 13/194 needs Lindelöf or random-matrix moments; uniform isolation needs pair correlation. Both are conditional in the source.
  6. Re-verification of the closed-item table. The closures are labels, not arguments. A reader who wants a proof must follow each label into publication.html; this dossier does not claim to have done so.
  7. Formalising the tracking theorem. T1/T7 are the algebraic skeleton of the off-line dichotomy. The step from the monomial product to ηN(ρ)ηN(1 − ρ) → 1/4 is an analytic theorem about ηN and is left as a source claim.

Python layer

python/programme.py implements the exact computation layer with sympy:

  • chord(N, s), chord_closed(N), chord_product(N, s);
  • chord_product_limit();
  • north(N, δ), south(N, δ), pole_ratio(N, δ);
  • product_sequence(n_max).

It raises ValueError for N < 1 and for product_sequence(n_max) with n_max < 2. No side effects on import.

Lean layer

lean/Topic.lean imports Mathlib, opens the namespace GapInventory, and proves T1–T7. The proofs use Real.rpow_add, Real.rpow_neg, Real.one_lt_rpow, field simplification, and elementary filter limits. There is no sorry, axiom, or admit. T2, T4, and T7 are not rfl padding: T2 is the degenerate-scale evaluation, T4 is a genuine positivity statement, and T7 combines the exponent law with strict one-sided bounds.
