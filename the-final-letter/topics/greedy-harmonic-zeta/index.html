Greedy harmonic zeta

A reconstructed research programme: finite formal core, exact computation, archived claims

Status. Archived claim with a reconstructed finite formal core. The source's Riemann-hypothesis reformulation is not proved here and is not a theorem of this reconstruction. The full dossier is in README.md.

Provenance and status

The primary source is rh-reformulation.html; the same archive directory contains an addendum, an eta-critical review, and the v2/v3 provability papers (21 files in all). The inventory is Scraps item 13 and the folder index is topics/.

The primary document defines a greedy condition on the partial sums of the Dirichlet eta function and asserts an RH equivalence (Theorems 4.1 and 5.1). The addendum explicitly gives no proof. The v3 paper advertises a rigorous proof of the critical exponent α = 1/2. All of these remain archived source claims here.

Three problems found while reconstructing

  1. Sign inconsistency. Definition 2.1 asks for Re(conj(S_{N-1}) w_N) ≤ 0 for every N, although the alternating series subtracts w_N at even N. The condition for the alternating sign itself to be greedy is (-1)^(N-1) Re(conj(S_{N-1}) w_N) ≤ 0.
  2. The first zero fails both finite readings. At t_1 ≈ 14.1347251417 the raw condition first fails at N = 4, and the sign-corrected alternating condition fails at N = 2. These are high-precision computations, not disproofs of RH; they show the source's finite condition is not satisfied as written.
  3. The analytic proof is circular or unsupported. Theorem 4.1 Part II uses the absence of zeros for σ > 1/2 (essentially RH), and the appendix replaces estimates with “one can show”. v3 likewise assumes a zero-free half-plane in its α > 1/2 step.

Open items and gaps

  1. A correct finite greedy statement. The source must decide whether w_N is signed; the first zero satisfies neither the raw nor the sign-corrected condition for all N. A repaired formulation, and its relation to RH, is open.
  2. Complex eta–zeta identity. T3 is the real, even-N truncation; analytic continuation and 1 − 2^(1−s) are not formalised.
  3. Analytic equivalence. Riemann–Siegel, the approximate functional equation, uniform errors, and phase density are outside Lean. The source appendix is not a proof.
  4. Audit of v3. The critical-exponent argument needs independent verification; its key step assumes the desired zero-free half-plane.
  5. Energy monotonicity. The addendum's claim is unproved and not in Lean.
  6. Relation to item 11. The shared name “greedy harmonic” is thematic; item 11 concerns angle decomposition, not eta/Dirichlet exponents. The folders are not duplicates.
