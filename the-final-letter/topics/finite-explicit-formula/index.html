Finite explicit formula

Status: partial. A fixed test function and its autocorrelation are certified, and the finite prime side is computed in rational interval arithmetic. The analytic explicit formula, the archimedean term and the zero side are open.

Target of status.html §7 P4: a certified finite-window explicit formula for a fixed test function, replacing floating point in B5.

The test function

The tent function f(x)=max(0,1-|x|) is even, non-negative and supported on [-1,1]. Its autocorrelation is the cubic B-spline

g(x) =  |x|^3/2 - |x|^2 + 2/3          (|x| ≤ 1)
       -|x|^3/6 + |x|^2 - 2|x| + 4/3   (1 ≤ |x| ≤ 2)
       0                                (|x| ≥ 2)

g is even and non-negative; its Fourier transform is ĝ(t)=|f̂(t)|²≥0.
