The Z'/Z growth bound

Status: the vertical input is proved unconditionally on the library contour Re s = 2 and Re s = −1; the programme is complete for the explicit-formula vertical sides.

Part of the topic index, the weil-positivity hub, the execution handoff, the O2 handoff, and The Final Letter.

Programme

Supply the Stirling-type bound ‖(Z'/Z)(x+iy)‖ ≤ M(1+|y|) and measurability, so that the harvested verticalLogDerivIntegrable_of_linear_bound proves VerticalLogDerivIntegrable.

The core also proves that any polynomial bound ‖(Z'/Z)(x+iy)‖ ≤ M(1+|y|)^k suffices, because every polynomial is dominated by the Gaussian test function. The linear theorem is the case k = 1. Since Stirling estimates naturally give polynomial bounds, this widens the usable interface.

Measurability is no longer a hypothesis. The core proves that ζ, the completed zeta Z and Z'/Z are measurable, and that the vertical-line product (Z'/Z)·H_c is AEStronglyMeasurable.

The growth interface is wider still: any bound ‖Z'/Z‖ ≤ M exp(A y²) with A < σ² also gives VerticalLogDerivIntegrable, because the Gaussian test function dominates it. This matters because Stirling/Gamma estimates naturally produce exponential-in-y² (or y log y) bounds, not just polynomials.
