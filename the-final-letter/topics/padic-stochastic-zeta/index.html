p-adic and stochastic zeta

A reconstructed research programme: p-adic formal core, exact computation, source claims, open bridges

Status. Adjacent reconstruction with a small formal core. The p-adic norm, finite encoding, local Euler factor, and the k = 2 Kubota–Leopoldt bookkeeping are reconstructed. The Kubota–Leopoldt construction, p-adic analyticity, stochastic representations, and p-adic spectral analysis remain open. The full dossier, theorem ledger, and provenance are in README.md.

Provenance and source assessment

The source is archive/essays/p-adic.html, “p-adic Numbers and the Kubota–Leopoldt p-adic Zeta Function”, attributed in the file to Grok 3 (xAI). It is a short, AI-generated expository essay introducing the p-adic norm, finite base-p encodings, p-adic geometry and calculus in outline, and stating the Kubota–Leopoldt interpolation formula

ζp(1−k) = (1 − pk−1) ζ(1−k).

It is not a research paper: it contains no proof of Kubota–Leopoldt existence, continuity or analyticity, and its “forex price” and schemes discussion is speculative framing with no mathematical content. The essay contains no stochastic process, no stochastic representation of zeta, and no p-adic spectral analysis. Those phrases belong to the inventory label for item 41, not to the essay. This dossier reconstructs only the local objects the essay actually defines.

The essay makes no claim about the Riemann hypothesis, GRH, twin primes, Chowla or Sarnak. No such claim is attributed to it here.

What T1–T7 do not mean. T1–T3 formalise the norm statements the essay uses as definitions or standard facts. T4 is exactly its worked encoding example. T5 is the finite geometric identity behind the local Euler factor, not the analytic Euler product. T6–T7 are the rational bookkeeping obtained by substituting ζ(−1) = −1/12 into the stated formula at k = 2. They are not a construction of the Kubota–Leopoldt function and do not prove its interpolation property for general k.
