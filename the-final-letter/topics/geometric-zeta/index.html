Geometric zeta — the decompositions, and what they do not buy

Status: Phase 0. The three exact decompositions are stated and audited; the Euler and Lambert identities are re-exported in Lean, the zero-free-factor structure is proved, the finite-product impossibility no_finite_euler_product is Lean-proved, and the numerical layer checks all three identities. The Lambert/Mellin transform Γ(s)ζ(s)² = ∫ t^(s−1)L(e^(−t))dt is now proved in Lean (lambert_mellin), and a new lower-bound expansion for ζ(σ)² is derived from it below. The RH-relevance audit is a documented no-go (RH-AUDIT.md): the half-plane inequality carries no zero information. No Riemann-hypothesis claim and no positivity claim.

The objects

For an integer a ≥ 2, the geometric zeta is g_a(s) = Σm≥1 a−ms = 1/(as − 1) on Re s > 0. Each is zero-free, and its poles are s = 2πik/log a, all on Re s = 0.

The additive index set is essential. Over all bases, at s = 2, 1 + Σa≥21/(a2−1) = 7/4, while ζ(2) = π2/6 ≈ 1.6449. The non-perfect-powers are the unique base system for which the geometric pieces tile ℤ≥2 exactly once.

What the audit rules out

  • Finitely many factors are impossible. A finite sum cannot equal ζ (as s → +∞ the sum → 0 while ζ(s) → 1); a finite product cannot equal ζ (at s = 1 the product is finite while ζ has a pole).
  • No finite stage sees a zero. Each geometric factor is zero-free, and a finite Euler product is zero-free wherever it is defined. Zeros are a collective effect of the infinite product's non-uniform convergence in the critical strip.
  • The additive route does not continue. The terms have poles on the imaginary axis and the sum converges only for Re s > 1.
  • No zero information. The decompositions are rearrangements of the same Dirichlet series.

A new lower-bound expansion

From the Mellin identity and the AM–GM inequality k² + m² ≥ 2km, for σ > 1 and J ≥ 0:

ζ(σ)² ≥ 2σ Σj=0J Γ(σ+j)/(j! Γ(σ)) · Ej(σ+j), with Ej(τ) = Σk,m≥1 (k−m)2j/(k²+m²)τ.

This is an integral/lattice-sum bound, not a coefficientwise Dirichlet-series comparison. The j=0 term is the AM–GM bound; each higher term improves it, and the numerical ratios to ζ(σ)² converge upward to 1 (at σ=2: 0.627 at J=0, 0.866 at J=4). Derivation, numerical table and Lean targets: INEQUALITY.md. The integrated J = 0,1 lattice bound is accepted as a paper/numerical result: the Mellin-substitution formalisation met a coercion blocker, and that decision is recorded rather than papered over.

RH-relevance audit: a no-go

The inequality is unconditional but has no zero content. Writing E0(s) for the quadrant lattice sum and D(s) = ζ(s)² − 2sE0(s), the Epstein functional equation gives a continuation relation for E0; D is non-negative only for real σ > 1, and at a nontrivial zero ρ one has D(ρ) = −2ρE0(ρ). The half-plane inequality therefore controls neither E0(ρ) nor the sign of the continued D on the critical line. This route is closed as a zero-exclusion mechanism; the precise missing ingredient is recorded in RH-AUDIT.md.

The next phase

Acceptance bar for a headline statement. It must be proved unconditionally, have content beyond a rearrangement, encode no zero location, and be formalisable without sorry. The two most promising directions are unconditional bounds from the Mellin/Lambert representation with positive coefficients d(n) ≥ 0, and a sharpened arithmetic consequence of the additive base decomposition. If neither yields content beyond the known, the documented negative result is the honest deliverable.
