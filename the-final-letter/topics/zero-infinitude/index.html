Infinitude of the nontrivial zeros

Status: initiated; formal core Lean-checked; ZeroTailSummable is reduced to infinitude alone, which remains open.

Part of the topic index, the weil-positivity hub, the execution handoff, the O2 handoff, and The Final Letter.

Programme

Supply the missing infinitude of the nontrivial zeros and feed ZeroTailSummable. Countability, isolatedness, local finiteness, the zero-count bound and inverse-square summability are already Lean-checked in absolute-polarized-hodge-index-realization.

The linear log-growth and ordinate-growth inputs of earlier revisions are discharged. The unconditional order-one bound exists_bound_log_norm_xi₀ gives inverse-square summability of the zero weight; summability forces the weight terms to zero, hence |Im(a n)| → ∞. The capstone exists_enumeration_and_zeroTailSummable_of_infinite therefore takes only infinitude and σ > 0.
