Weil positivity — research programme

Status: updated in the light of the missing-bridge investigation. Finite on-line positivity and the exact isolated-block criterion are Lean-checked. The isolated-block blocks ⇒ RH implication is vacuous as a bridge (its antecedent is refutable), and the full-block route has an unproven maximal-|δ| precondition. The classical route is the arithmetic side, now reduced in missing-bridge to the exact identity K = PNT errors + arch. Unconditional Weil positivity is open and RH-equivalent.

Part of the topic index, the O2 handoff, the execution plan, and The Final Letter.

Programme

Weil positivity (objective O2) is the statement that the zero-side Gram matrices of the Gaussian test family are positive semidefinite. It is the hub of three upstream programmes: zero-infinitude supplies the enumeration and zero tail — its ordinate-growth and log-growth inputs are discharged, so ZeroTailSummable follows from infinitude alone; log-derivative-growth supplies the Z'/Z growth bound — its Gaussian upper half and measurability are proved, and only the Gamma lower bound remains; zero-density-bound supplies the entrywise tail bound, with the count, exact obstruction and height tail proved. The order of execution and acceptance criteria are in HANDOFF.md.

The isolated block is [[S(0), S(c)], [S(c), S(0)]], and the core proves the exact scalar criterion OffLineBlockPSD c σ δ γ ↔ |S(c)| ≤ S(0). The obstruction therefore reads: an off-line zero forces some separation c with |S(c)| > S(0). The converse assembly is not claimed.

Correction from missing-bridge. This isolated-block implication is not the bridge. not_offLineBlockPSD_of_offLine proves that every off-line quadruple has a separation with a non-PSD block, so the antecedent ∀ c σ δ γ, δ ≠ 0 → OffLineBlockPSD c σ δ γ is false and no_offLine_of_all_blocks_psd is vacuously true. The meaningful objects are the full Gram matrix and FiniteWeilPositivity; the easy direction RH → FiniteWeilPositivity is proved in missing-bridge (T20), and the hard direction is O4.

Corrections and dependencies from missing-bridge

The missing-bridge investigation changes the reading of this hub in four places.

  • The isolated-block bridge is vacuous. no_offLine_of_all_blocks_psd has the antecedent ∀ c σ δ γ, δ ≠ 0 → OffLineBlockPSD c σ δ γ, which is refuted by not_offLineBlockPSD_of_offLine. The theorem is true but it is False → RH; it is not the Weil positivity ⇒ RH direction in any non-vacuous sense.
  • The full statement is FiniteWeilPositivity. The hub's WeilPositivity is the on-line kernel identity for arbitrary real frequencies; the classical object is the full zero-side Gram matrix. Missing-bridge proves rh_implies_finiteWeilPositivity (the easy direction) and leaves the hard direction FiniteWeilPositivity → RH as O4.
  • The full-block route needs a strict maximal-|δ| zero. no_uniform_bound_of_offLine_quadruple shows that an off-line quadruple in the “other” block defeats every uniform bound; the domination argument therefore requires the chosen quadruple to have strictly larger |δ| than every other zero. No such zero is known.
  • The arithmetic side is the classical route. Missing-bridge reduces the arithmetic Gram entry to K(c) = [Pmain − P](c) + [Pmain − P](−c) + arch(c): the pole cancels exactly, and the PNT error and archimedean term are individually indefinite but jointly positive (C15–C17). The APM does not retire the tail bound. The remaining analytic input is a uniform PNT-error + archimedean bound.

What should change here. The ledger rows T1–T13 remain true as formal statements, but the hub should not present the isolated-block implication as the bridge. The bridge is O4 (the classical Weil criterion) on the arithmetic side; the zero-side domination route is not viable as stated.

Open items and known gaps

  • Unconditional Weil positivity — RH-equivalent; no unconditional proof is claimed.
  • Assembly — combine ZeroTailSummable, VerticalLogDerivIntegrable and FullBlockWithin into the full zero-side positivity statement.
  • Full Gram matrix and the hard direction — the finite easy direction is proved in missing-bridge (rh_implies_finiteWeilPositivity); the hard direction FiniteWeilPositivity → RH is O4 and is RH-strength.
  • Maximal-|δ| precondition — the full-block domination route requires a strict maximal-|δ| zero; no such zero is known (missing-bridge, T17).
  • Arithmetic side — the classical route is the explicit formula evaluated on pole + prime + archimedean terms; missing-bridge has the exact identity and the cancellation, and the open input is a uniform PNT-error + archimedean bound. This is the recommended route.
  • Mutual reliance — the O2 dossier angular-partition-method owns the status and ledger; the Lean core lives in absolute-polarized-hodge-index-realization. Keep both links current.
