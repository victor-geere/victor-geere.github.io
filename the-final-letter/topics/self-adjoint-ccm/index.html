Self-adjoint / CCM operator

A reconstructed research programme: elementary formal core, exact computation, source claims

Status. Speculative source blueprint; reconstructed elementary core and computation layer. Provenance and status of the source

The archived paper, The Generalised Riemann Helix and the Connes–Consani–Moscovici Spectral Triple: A Geometric Blueprint for the Hilbert–Pólya Programme, is a blueprint, not a proof. It (1) defines a classical curve whose angular phase is tuned to the non-trivial zeros of an L-function; (2) writes down a Dirac operator Dλ with point interactions at the prime logarithms m log p and asserts that it is the self-adjoint realisation of the CCM scaling operator; (3) reduces the CCM Conjecture 1 to WKB asymptotics and a finite-dimensional trace formula — the two halves of Gap 3 — with spectrum identification as Gap 4. The source's Gap 1 (phase construction) and Gap 2 (rigorous Dirac operator) remain source claims here.

The paper is explicit that RH and GRH are not proved by it. Its phase φ is built from zeros assumed to lie on the critical line (GRH) and to be enumerated there. The dossier therefore separates what can be proved from the paper's own definitions (a small calculus and bookkeeping core), what the paper asserts (self-adjointness, the CCM matrix identification, the trace formula, the torsion distribution), and what remains open (Conjecture 1, WKB estimates, trace convergence, spectrum identification).

Scope caution. The inventory note calls this "the largest genuine research corpus". That describes the file count of the surrounding directory (the rh1–rh25 sequence, torus papers, Lean files), not the logical status of this paper. The source page used here is a single speculative blueprint.

Weil-positivity bridge: the short version

The bridge survey asks which dossier can reach W(g) ≥ 0. The chain is: a self-adjoint operator D with spectrum {γ_n}, its spectral measure Σ δγ_n, an identification W(f*f̌) = ∫ ĝ dμD, and positivity. The last step is formal; the identification is the bridge.

WEIL-BRIDGE.md develops the CCM candidate through this chain and records two finite obstructions. First, with the blueprint's constant matching condition, the spectrum of Dλ is the arithmetic progression (Θ + 2πn)/L (or the antiperiodic shift), independent of the prime positions; it cannot be the zero set. Second, the formal band-limited compression Qkℓ = ⟨ek, Dλ eℓ⟩ has diagonal ε μk + S/L; the corner Q−N,−N diverges linearly and is negative for all sufficiently large N. The compression is therefore not positive semidefinite and cannot be the positive CCM Weil matrix.

What survives is a genuine finite core — Hermiticity (T8), the exact diagonal (T9), positivity of the prime/delta part (T10), and unitarity of the matching product (T11) — together with a sharp filter on repairs. The recommended finite step is a Weil Gram matrix on a smooth test basis, as in prime-spectrum-tensor; a first numerical 4×4 instance with an active prime term is in WEIL-GRAM.md. The operator repair needs an energy-dependent matching, which the displayed equation does not have.

Open items and gaps

  1. CCM Conjecture 1 (S1). The central open statement; nothing here bears on its truth.
  2. WKB asymptotics (Gap 3a). Uniform resolvent analysis of Dλ near zero energy with many weak point interactions; not attempted.
  3. Trace-formula convergence (Gap 3b). The source expects an O(L−1/3+ε) error via Krein's spectral shift function; not proved.
  4. Spectrum identification (Gap 4). Conditional on 2 and 3; not proved.
  5. Derivation of the torsion formula in Lean. torsionFormula is a definition taken from the source; T2–T4 study it, but the equality with the Frenet–Serret torsion of helix is only checked symbolically in Python (C2). A Lean derivation needs a ℝ³ cross-product/Frenet development.
  6. Self-adjointness (S3). Even the elementary unitary matching condition (T5) does not give a self-adjoint operator; deficiency indices, domains, and resolvents are outside this core.
  7. Distributional torsion (S2) and the smooth background. The bump construction and its limit are not formalised.
  8. The 2N + 1 dimension of EN(λ) is stated in the source and is elementary, but is not included in the Lean core because it plays no role in the torsion analysis below.
  9. Bridge obstruction (constant matching). For the displayed matching condition, the spectrum is a lattice independent of the prime positions; this excludes S7 as written: S3 may still hold, but it cannot deliver the zeros. See WEIL-BRIDGE.md §2.
  10. Compression is not the positive Weil matrix. Its diagonal has a negative corner for large N (T9, C9–C10); the delta part alone is PSD (T10). A repair must renormalise or replace the compression, not merely symmetrise it.
  11. Test-space mismatch. The half-integer modes are antiperiodic and lie outside the Weil cone, so QW(ek,eℓ) is not defined without a regularisation. S4 is ill-posed as stated.
  12. Energy-dependent matching. The only operator repair that escapes the lattice no-go is a genuinely energy-dependent scattering matrix from a different quantisation; none is constructed here.

What would change the verdict

The programme would become a mathematical proof of RH only if S1–S7 were proved, and in particular if the WKB estimate and the trace-formula convergence were established with the stated error bounds. The bridge development shows a prior obstruction: S4/S7 fail for the displayed constant-matching operator, and S3—even if proved—would only give a lattice; proving S1 alone would not close the bridge. The dossier would reopen if an energy-dependent matching or a renormalised operator with a bounded diagonal and the correct spectral measure were constructed (see WEIL-BRIDGE.md §6). Until then this folder documents a geometric reduction with a verified elementary core. The honest summary is: the helix and its torsion formula are real and checkable; the operator is a proposal whose spectrum is a lattice; the positivity bridge is blocked as written.
