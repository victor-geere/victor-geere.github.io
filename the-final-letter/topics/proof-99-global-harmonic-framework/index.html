Proof-99 / Global Harmonic Framework

A reconstructed research programme: finite formal core, exact computation, archived claims

Status: archived claim. The source claims a proof of the Riemann Hypothesis. This folder does not treat that claim as proved. It formalises the finite coefficient and determinant bookkeeping that the source actually defines, and records the global argument as an archived source claim. The full dossier is in README.md.

Honesty note. The source’s claimed proof of RH, the Mayer–Ruelle similarity theorem, the determinant identity involving ζ, the Birman–Krein step, and the conclusion N₀(T) = N(T) are not theorems of this reconstruction. They appear below as Source claim or Open. In addition, the source’s claim that its kernel K is symmetric is false for the K it defines; an exact computation of the asymmetry defect is included.

What the source constructs

ObjectDefinition
First coefficient sequencea n = 1 / sqrt(n(n+1)), n ≥ 1
Second coefficient sequenceb n = 1 / (n+1), n ≥ 1
GHD correlation kernelK = I + |a⟩⟨b|, i.e. K u = u + a · Σ bₘ uₘ
Diagonal weightingD(s) = diag((n+2)^(−(s+1/2)))
Weighted Gram operatorM(s) = D(s) K D(s)
Critical-line familyM_t = M(1/2 + it)
Similarity target𝓛_s f(x) = Σ_{n≥1} (x+n)^(−2s) f(1/(x+n))
Claimed determinantdet(I − M(s)) = ζ(s)/ζ(2s) · e^{P(s)}
Mismatch functionA_J(t) = −d/dt arg F_reg(t)
Counting functionsN₀(T) critical-line zeros; N(T) all non-trivial zeros

The source’s chain is: define K and M(s); prove M(s) trace-class; claim unitary equivalence to 𝓛_s; invoke the classical determinant formula; restrict to the critical line; identify the spectral shift with A_J; compute ∫A_J two ways; conclude N₀(T) = N(T); infer RH. Only the first, finite step is reconstructed here.

Open items

  1. Is there a genuinely symmetric finite kernel? The natural repair is K_sym = I + ½(|a⟩⟨b| + |b⟩⟨a|); no source document analyses it.
  2. Does the similarity theorem hold? No complete proof exists in the repository.
  3. Can N₀(T) = N(T) be derived from the stated asymptotics? A uniform error term is needed; an integer difference plus a growing O(log T) bound is insufficient as stated.
  4. What is the precise relation between the GHF kernel and the Mayer–Ruelle operator? Even granting the determinant identity, the spectra are not identified.
  5. Hilbert–Pólya. The source itself lists the eigenvalue identification as unproved.

The weighted determinant identity used is det(D K D) = (Πₙ dₙ²)(1 + Σₙ aₙbₙ) with dₙ = (n+2)^(−(s+1/2)): the finite matrix determinant lemma applied to M(s) = D² + (Da)(bᵀD). The correction is independent of s because the weights cancel in bᵀD⁻²Da.
