Factorisation and theta

A reconstructed research programme: finite formal core, exact computation, and an open theta–zeta bridge

Status. Speculative essay vein. The finite detector algebra and finite theta sums are reconstructed and checked; the theta–zeta bridge and every analytic limit remain source claims or open items. Full provenance, theorem ledger, and usage are in README.md.

Provenance and source assessment

The source is a single short HTML essay, “The Theta Function and Its Mathematical Connections” (about 10 KB, seven prose sections). It has no numbered theorem environments, no proofs beyond substitution and differentiation, and no bibliography. Its content is a chain: the divisor detector, the claim that its roots are exactly the integer divisors of k, the classical theta function, a Mellin-transform sketch of the zeta functional equation, and a finite divisor theta sum. The essay itself concedes that a direct map from the detector to theta is “elusive” and that the correspondence “remains indirect”.

It is therefore an expository, speculative vein, not a proof of new arithmetic. Nothing in it states or proves the Riemann hypothesis. The only bridge to zeta is the standard theta–Mellin derivation, quoted without convergence or continuation details. This dossier formalises the genuine elementary layer and keeps the analytic bridge explicitly open.

One erratum: the source writes the derivative of cos(2πk/x) with a leading minus sign. The correct derivative is +(2πk/x²) sin(2πk/x). The conclusion f'(d)=0 at a divisor is unaffected, and the computation layer certifies the correct second derivative.

Open items and gaps

  1. No zeta or RH theorem. The source does not state RH and proves nothing about the zeros of ζ. The theta–Mellin route is the ordinary classical construction; it is not a new proof of the functional equation, and it says nothing about the critical line.
  2. Real-root characterisation. Extend T5 from integer inputs to all real x ≠ 0 in Lean, using Real.cos_eq_one_iff twice.
  3. Exact multiplicity in general. Formalise f_k''(d) = −4π²((k/d)²/d² + 1) ≠ 0 for all positive divisors, not only the tested k = 12 cases.
  4. Infinite theta algebra. Formalise convergence of θ(t), its positivity, and the functional equation. This needs Poisson summation or the classical modular transformation.
  5. Mellin/zeta bridge. Formalise the Mellin transform computation and the zeta functional equation, including convergence and continuation. Deliberately left open.
  6. The f_k–θ dictionary. The source itself concedes that no precise mapping is known. Any such dictionary would be a new conjecture, not a result of this essay.
