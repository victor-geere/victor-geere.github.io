Digamma asymptotics and the archimedean contour shift

Status: the digamma expansion, the asymptotic bounds and the archimedean contour shift for the Gaussian test are proved. Nothing here proves the Riemann hypothesis.

This project addresses the blocker for the explicit formula in archimedean-prime-evaluation: moving the archimedean vertical integral from Re s = 2 to Re s = 1/2.

Provenance

The key input exists in intersection-form-zeta/lean/DigammaEnclosure.lean: the Euler–Maclaurin expansion ψ(w) = log w − 1/(2w) − 1/(12w²) + 1/(120w⁴) − R(w) and the bound ‖R(w)‖ ≤ 1/(81 (Re w)^5). These are harvested here as T1/T2.

Objects

  • archFactor s = (1/2) log π − (1/2) ψ(s/2);
  • ArchimedeanShift H — the shift identity for a test function.
