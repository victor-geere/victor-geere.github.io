Provenance, and why this is a notes dossier

The inventory lists item 36 as “notes duplicate”. The folder holds the working drafts behind item 35 (QEFE), not a separate result: the rounds repeat, qualify, and in places retract one another. The v8 draft is the most polished member of the set and is the source for this reconstruction, but it is still a draft:

  • Round 1 argues the opposite of round 8. research-deepseek-1.md derives a relation Ψ(s) ~ Ψ(s−1), observes that a finite Dirichlet polynomial cannot satisfy a Riemann-type s ↔ 1−s equation, and writes “clearly the functional equation cannot hold for all s”. Round 8 asserts Theorem 3.1 with s ↔ 1−s and does not address that objection.
  • The v8 proof is interrupted. In §5.1 the Mellin split is followed to the point where it “merely reproduces the original first part”; the text then says the equation follows “after a different manipulation”, which is never supplied.
  • The Mellin normalisation changes silently. §2.4 uses Γ(s) π−s D2s; §5.3 replaces it by Γ((s+a)/2) π−(s+a)/2 Ds by hand, with the parity a asserted rather than derived. Computation C4 measures the mismatch.
  • The examples are only checked at s = 1/2. At that point the prefactor is exactly 1, so the check is automatic (C5); away from the centre the displayed equation fails for the source's own Ising and SU(2)4 data, as recorded in item 35.

The primary reconstruction already covers the general QEFE, the worked examples, the off-centre failure, and the root-number inconsistency. This dossier therefore does not re-derive them. It adapts the finite Egyptian/fractional core — the one part of the notes that is unconditional — and records the duplicated and contradictory material honestly.

Reconstructed objects and notation

The finite input is an integral MTC datum: positive squared quantum dimensions di² with d0² = 1, total D² = Σi di², and Galois signs χi = εi(σ) ∈ {±1} with ε0 = 1.

Lemma 2.2 of the source, Σi≠0 1/mi = 1 − 1/D², is the bridge between the tables above. Its general proof is the finite identity proved in ../qefe/lean/Topic.lean (QEFE.egyptian_total, QEFE.egyptian_fraction_identity); this folder adapts the surrounding monotonicity and positivity facts and verifies the two worked examples exactly.

Python layer

python/programme.py is a small exact layer built with sympy only:

  • Datum — validated finite datum (positive squared dimensions, unit object, signs ±1), with properties D2, denominators, reciprocals, total_mass, nonunit_mass;
  • nonunit_mass(D2) — the closed form 1 − 1/D²;
  • categorical_psi(datum, s) — the finite Dirichlet polynomial;
  • mellin_factor_section_2_4, mellin_factor_section_5_3, normalisation_ratio — the two competing normalisations;
  • qefe_prefactor(D2, a, s) — the prefactor of the claimed equation;
  • ISING, SU2_4, example(name) — the two worked examples.

All computations are exact rational or gamma expressions; sympy.N is used only to quote the normalisation ratio. No mpmath dependency is needed.

Open items and known gaps

  1. The central gap is the normalisation, not a missing epsilon. Reconcile §2.4's Γ(s) π−s D2s with §5.3's Γ((s+a)/2) π−(s+a)/2 Ds, and derive the parity a from the T-matrix instead of asserting it. Until then no QEFE theorem can be stated precisely.
  2. Resolve round 1 versus round 8. Either exhibit an infinite family of categorical data whose finite polynomials converge to a Riemann-type object, or accept the earlier note's conclusion that the finite polynomial has no such functional equation.
  3. Formalise the Galois sign identity. Appendix A is a citation, not a proof. A formal version would need the de Boer–Goeree / Coste–Gannon / Bantay equivariance.
  4. Theta modularity. Formalise the lattice realisation and Poisson summation behind Lemma 4.2, including rank and determinant hypotheses.
  5. The SU(2)k → ζ(2s) limit. State a precise normalised limit theorem and prove it, or explain why the Galois signs obstruct it. No RH consequence follows from the present material.
  6. Deduplication. Item 35 is the primary reconstruction. This folder should keep only the notes-specific finite core and the provenance record; further general QEFE work belongs in item 35.
