Partial sum plus tail — certified evaluation of ζ, ζ(s,a) and L(s,χ)

Objective III. The first two objectives of the Final Letter are the two walls (RH and parity). This one is the positive computation layer they need: a certified evaluator that returns a rational rectangle containing ζ(s), ζ(s,a) or L(s,χ), with a kernel-checked replay of every rational operation.

Source bundle: ~/workspace/rh/research/twin-prime-parity/012/ (“a partial-sum-plus-tail evaluator with checked certificates”), snapshotted in ~/workspace/rh/research/publish/013-twin-prime-parity-explicit-function/. The eta analysis is in the published bundle’s notes/004-eta-cos-sin.md.

What the programme is

For a > 0, b = a + M, rising factorial (s)n = s(s+1)…(s+n−1) and Bernoulli numbers B2j, Euler–Maclaurin gives

ζ(s,a) = Σ_{k<M} (a+k)^(-s)
       + b^(1-s)/(s-1) + b^(-s)/2
       + Σ_{j=1..K} B_{2j}/(2j)! · (s)_{2j-1} · b^(-s-2j+1)
       + R_K(s),

with the explicit tail bound

|R_K(s)| ≤ |(s)_{2K+1}| · 2ζ(2K+1)/(2π)^(2K+1) · b^(-σ-2K)/(σ+2K).

The Dirichlet L-function is reduced to Hurwitz zeta by

L(s,χ) = N^(-s) Σ_{a=1..N-1} χ(a) ζ(s, a/N).

Every factor is enclosed by exact rationals: π by Machin’s formula, log by the atanh series after a power-of-two reduction, exp by a reduced Taylor series, and sin/cos by Taylor after a rigorous 2π reduction. There is no floating point in the computation layer.

The evaluator records every rational operation into a straight-line certificate:

  • atoms — trusted leaves: the input s, rational constants, and the transcendental enclosures;
  • ops — the checkable cell: add, sub, neg, scale, mul, inflate, each with its declared output box.

The ops cell is replayed by an independent Python checker and kernel-checked in Lean: Expr.wf recomputes every box and Expr.wf_sound proves that an accepted certificate encloses the value it denotes.

The eta cos/sin split

The alternating handle used by the published 013 bundle is

η(s) = Σ_{n≥1} (−1)^(n−1) n^(-s) = (1 − 2^(1−s)) ζ(s).

Writing s = σ + it and φn = t log n,

Re η(σ+it) =  Σ_{n≥1} (−1)^(n−1) n^(-σ) cos(t log n),
Im η(σ+it) = −Σ_{n≥1} (−1)^(n−1) n^(-σ) sin(t log n).

The finite partial sums are formalised in lean/Topic.lean: etaPartial_re and etaPartial_im prove the split exactly. At the first zero the series does not vanish termwise — the cancellation is in the tail — so the evaluator’s tail bound, not the first hundred terms, is what certifies the enclosure.

Open items

  1. Euler–Maclaurin formula. The envelopes are proved and the atom boxes are discharged (cert_mem_closed). What remains is S1–S2: the identity that the replay value is ζ(s) and its explicit tail bound.
  2. Derivatives. partial_tail.py encloses values, not L'/L; adding derivatives costs a div soundness lemma and differentiated tail atoms.
  3. s = 1. The individual Hurwitz terms have a pole that cancels only in the Σ χ(a) sum; L(1,χ₃) is checked at 3/2 instead.
  4. Larger certificates. The 64-bit certificates are exact rationals; the largest in the source bundle is about 115 kB.
