Harmonic sine

A reconstructed research programme: geometric formal core, exact computation, archived RH claim

Status. Geometric core reconstructed and checked. The source's Riemann-hypothesis claim is archived and superseded; it is not a theorem here. The full dossier is in README.md.

Provenance and what this folder is

The designated source is the 9 KB ebook harmonic-sine-rh.html (7 March 2026), with the sibling geometric note sine-harmonic.html and its TeX source. The inventory entry is Scraps item 15, tagged archived claim and superseded.

The ebook's first half defines a greedy reconstruction of sin(πx) from harmonic steps π/(n+2) fused by the Pythagorean rotation rule. Its second half claims that a "harmonic sheaf" over the zeta landscape, plus an extension of Matomäki–Radziwiłł discorrelation to 1/n phases, yields the Riemann hypothesis. Only the finite geometry survives as mathematics that can be proved; the zeta/RH material is recorded as source claim or open item.

Erratum. The source defines h_n = √((1 − cos(π/(n+2)))/2), which is sin(π/(2(n+2))) — the half-angle — while the rotation update needs sin(π/(n+2)). The sibling sine-harmonic.tex instead uses dyadic_sin(1/(n+2)). The Python layer verifies both the exact half-angle identity and the discrepancy (C1, C2); Lean uses the correct (sin ε, cos ε) pair.

Errata

  1. Half-angle substitution. The source's h n is sin(ε n/2), while the rotation needs sin ε n; as written the recurrence advances by half the selected angle. The sibling note uses the full step sine instead.
  2. √(1 − s n²) is not cos θ n. It is |cos θ n| and flips sign after θ n > π/2. The sibling sine-harmonic.html repairs this by carrying c n forward; the Lean rotation is the repaired form.
  3. Same text, three files. harmonic-sine-rh.html, harmonic-sine-zeta.html and the essay-level copy are near-duplicates; they are not independent evidence.

Python layer

python/programme.py uses sympy for exact rational/trigonometric work and mpmath (50 digits) for the rotation reconstruction. The greedy layer works in exact rational multiples of π, so residual comparisons never round. It implements harmonic_angle, half_angle_sine, step_sine, rotation_step, greedy_word, greedy_residuals, residual_bound_holds, and reconstruct_sine.

Open items and gaps

  1. Full greedy recursion. Formalise the finite sequence θ n, prove 0 ≤ r n ≤ π/(n+1) by induction, and derive r n → 0. T7 is only the one-step induction step.
  2. Reconstruction limit. Combine a sine Lipschitz estimate with r n → 0 and the reflection y ↦ 2 − y to obtain the source's boxed identity.
  3. Resolve the h n erratum across the v2/v3 files and the geometric note, and state the corrected recurrence once.
  4. Precise zeta correspondence. The source's table is an analogy with no explicit map or identity to prove.
  5. 1/n-phase discorrelation. The claimed Matomäki–Radziwiłł extension has no complete proof in the source; it remains open and unformalised.
  6. The RH claim. Harmonic-sheaf acyclicity is an archived, superseded claim. RH is not proved here.
