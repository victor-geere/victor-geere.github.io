The W4 zero-density entrywise bound

Status: initiated; the conditional 2×2 obstruction and the exact 2×2 PSD criterion are Lean-checked; the zero-density estimate supplying B is open.

Part of the topic index, the weil-positivity hub, the execution handoff, the O2 handoff, and The Final Letter.

Programme

Isolate the missing zero-density entrywise bound as a named tail input, then feed it to the harvested full-block obstruction. The unconditional 2×2 obstruction for a single off-line quadruple is already Lean-checked.

The core also proves the exact scalar criterion for the symmetric diagonal block: !![a, b; b, a] is PSD iff |b| ≤ a. That criterion is what makes the obstruction checkable by a single real inequality.

Open items

  • Zero-density estimate supplying a uniform B; the height-tail mass is already proved to vanish (T15).
  • Counting input: Riemann–von Mangoldt up to constants is proved (T10); the remaining density estimate must make the tail small uniformly in the height.
  • Extension from the 2×2 block to a full Gaussian Gram matrix.

Objects and notation

The objects are the isolated off-line quadruple {ρ, 1−ρ, conj ρ, 1−conj ρ}, its closed-form contribution S(c), the full zero-side Gram block T, and the tail block contributed by the other zeros.
