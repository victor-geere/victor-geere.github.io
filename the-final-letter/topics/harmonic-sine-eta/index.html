Harmonic sine and the eta function

A fragment reconstructed into a finite formal core, exact computation, and an honest ledger of source claims

Status. Fragment reconstructed into a small Lean core and an exact sympy layer. The archived source’s uniqueness claim for α = 1/2 is preserved as an archived/speculative source claim and is not a theorem of this reconstruction. Full dossier: README.md.

What the programme is

The source proposes a non-greedy harmonic sine reconstruction. It fixes the alternating signs (-1)^(n-1) and studies

S_α(θ) = Σ (-1)^(n-1) n^(-α) sin(θ log n)
C_α(θ) = Σ (-1)^(n-1) n^(-α) cos(θ log n)

These are the imaginary and real parts of the Dirichlet eta function η(α+iθ). The source then imposes a greedy angular condition on the partial sums S_N,

Re(conj(S_(N-1)) · w_N) ≤ 0 for all N,   w_N = N^(-α) e^(iθ log N),

together with η(α+iθ) = 0, and asserts that this forces α = 1/2.

This folder reconstructs the finite algebra the programme actually uses: positive decreasing weights, the period-two alternating sign, pairing of adjacent terms, and the finite factorisation of even eta truncations. Convergence, analytic continuation, zero-free regions and the uniqueness argument are recorded as source claims and open items.

Honesty note. The source is a short fragment. Its headline uniqueness assertion is not a proof, and its zero-free-region step is misstated. The genuine, checkable content is the finite alternating-series algebra formalised as T1–T7 and the exact computations C1–C8. Nothing here proves the Riemann hypothesis, and no archived claim is upgraded to a theorem.

Open items and known gaps

  1. Convergence and real/imaginary identification. Formalise convergence of S_α, C_α for α > 0 and the identity with η(α+iθ).
  2. Analytic continuation and functional equation. Needed for the claims about zeros with α < 1/2; neither is formalised.
  3. Zero-free region. Replace the source’s Re(s) > 1/2 assertion with the correct classical Re(s) > 1, then determine what survives for 1/2 < α ≤ 1.
  4. Greedy condition at zeros. Decide rigorously whether Re(conj(S_(N-1)) w_N) ≤ 0 holds at any zero, and whether it can select a unique exponent. The θ = 0 edge case shows the condition is not vacuous.
  5. Uniqueness of α = 1/2. No complete proof is given; excluding α < 1/2 without assuming the conclusion would need a quantitative Riemann–Siegel estimate.
  6. Relation to RH. Any claim that the admissible zeros are exactly the critical-line zeros is at least as strong as RH; it is not claimed here.

What is not formalised

  • Convergence of the sine/cosine series and identification with η.
  • Analytic continuation, functional equation, and zero-free regions.
  • The Riemann–Siegel formula and partial-sum estimates at zeros.
  • The greedy-condition characterisation and uniqueness of α = 1/2.
  • Any statement about RH, twin primes, Chowla, Sarnak, or Elliott.
