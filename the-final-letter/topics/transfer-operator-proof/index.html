Transfer-operator proof

A reconstructed research programme: finite formal core, exact computation, and the archived claims left open

Status. Finite formal core and exact computation layer reconstructed. The archived source is an exploratory programme, not a proof; the Riemann Hypothesis and the analytic continuation of the operator family remain open here and in the source.

Source: archive/maths/rh.html — “A Transfer Operator Proof of the Riemann Hypothesis — Final Version”.
Inventory: Scraps item 33 · Topic index.

What the programme is

The source proposes a Hilbert–Pólya strategy: realise the nontrivial zeros of ζ as the spectrum of a regularised transfer operator L̃s, equip the space with a Petersson pairing, prove self-adjointness and vanishing deficiency indices, and conclude that the spectrum is real, hence that every zero lies on Re s = ½. Parts VI–IX of the source then audit that strategy and conclude that each load-bearing step is either circular or equivalent to RH. The document ends, correctly, with the status open.

This folder does not reassert the strategy. It reconstructs the finite algebra the source actually defines — the diagonal Euler-product operator, its finite Fredholm determinant, the Euclidean self-adjointness of a real diagonal operator, and the indefinite Krein form of Definition 3.1 — and records everything analytic, spectral and RH-related as a source claim or an open item.

Honesty note. The source labels several statements “Theorem”, including the Riemann Hypothesis itself (its Theorem 5.2). Those are not theorems of this reconstruction. They appear in the source-claims table below with their archived status. Nothing here proves RH, Li's criterion, the explicit formula, or any zero-location statement.

The source itself corrects two earlier errors: ΨP(s) = ∏p sin(πs/p) is not a prime sieve (it vanishes at every integer n ≥ 2, composites included), and the Petersson pairing is an indefinite Krein form, not a positive inner product. Both corrections are preserved below.

Open items and known gaps

  1. RH itself. Nothing in this folder proves, disproves, or numerically resolves the Riemann Hypothesis. C8 checks a classical symmetry of ξ; it does not check the location of its zeros.
  2. Determinant completion. Construct a trace-class (or Hilbert–Schmidt det2) operator whose determinant is an entire completion of ξ, independently of the zeros. The source's A eρ = ρ−1 eρ is built from the zeros and is therefore circular; no trace-class realisation exists (source Thm. 6.3, not formalised).
  3. Metric positivity / unbroken PT. The source identifies this with the Weil–Li positivity criterion, which is equivalent to RH. It is not derived anywhere.
  4. Analytic continuation into ½ < Re s ≤ 1. The Euler-built operator converges only for Re s > 1; continuing the operator family is not the same as continuing 1/ζ. This remains open.
  5. Deficiency indices and spectral reality. Essential self-adjointness of the analytic L̃s is not established; the finite diagonal facts T5–T6 do not transfer to the infinite-dimensional, indefinite setting.
  6. de Bruijn–Newman gap. The unconditional bounds leave Λ ∈ [0, 0.22]; showing Λ = 0 is exactly RH.
