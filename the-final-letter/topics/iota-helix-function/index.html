Iota helix function

A reconstructed finite formal core, an exact computation layer, and the source claims left open

Status. The source is a speculative AI-generated fragment. This reconstruction proves the finite helix and eta identities that it actually defines, computes the finite greedy signs exactly, and leaves the geometric Riemann-hypothesis route open. The full dossier is README.md.

Provenance and status

Source: iota-framework.html, with the helix notation taken from the companion iota-function.tex. Inventory: Scraps item 18 · Topic index.

The archived folder is a fragment of overlapping drafts, not a completed programme. iota-framework.html reads the Dirichlet eta series

η(s) = Σn≥1 (−1)n−1 n−s,   s = σ + it

as a planar walk whose steps are the individual terms; the companion note adds a three-dimensional helix. Neither file proves anything about the zeros of η or ζ. The claim that the “greedy condition” is equivalent to RH is an archived source claim, not a theorem of this reconstruction.

Two source inconsistencies. (1) The framework defines vn = (−1)n−1 n−s but writes its components with +sin(t log n), i.e. ns̄ instead of n−s; the companion note's phase Φn = π(n−1) − t log n is the consistent choice. (2) The companion note's greedy pairing uses wN = N−s without the alternating sign, whereas the actual step is vN = (−1)N−1 N−s; computation C8 exhibits the discrepancy.

Open items

  1. GC ⇒ critical line. The central unresolved claim. A precise statement with the correct sign (C8) and a proof that it forces σ = 1/2 remain open.
  2. Convergence and zeros. Convergence of the complex eta series, analytic continuation, and the relation to ζ are not formalised.
  3. Continuous GC. Define Σ(τ) = ∫1τ eiπ(u−1) u−s du and relate its inequality to the discrete pairing.
  4. Injectivity. Settle the phase-unwrapping issue in the recovery formula.
  5. Arc length. Prove the claimed finite/infinite trichotomy at σ = 1.
  6. Sign conventions. Decide whether the principal object is n−s (used here) or its conjugate (framework components), and restate the source claims in one convention.
  7. GC is not vacuous. C7 shows the pairing already alternates sign on the real axis; any RH-facing statement must explain the critical-line difference.
