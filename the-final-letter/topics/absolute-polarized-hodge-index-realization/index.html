Status discipline. This is a reconstruction: the archived claim (that an absolute Hodge index would give RH) is recorded as a source claim, not as a theorem. The theorem ledger separates what is proved, computed, claimed and open. No RH proof yet.

The target

Construct a proper polarized arithmetic/adelic surface — or the strongest rigorous substitute — whose intersection pairing

\[ \langle D(f),D(f)\rangle = Z(f)\cdot Z(f) \]

computes the CCM scaling-correspondence pairing and is negative definite on degree-zero classes. Then the Yuan–Zhang arithmetic Hodge index theorem would give

\[ Z(f)\cdot Z(f)\le 0 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad \operatorname{Tr}\bigl(\vartheta_m(f\star f^\#)\mid H^1\bigr)\ge0 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad \mathrm{RH}. \]

The open realization problem is recorded in ../intersection-form-zeta/notes/07-riemann-roch-square.md §4.2 and ../intersection-form-zeta/notes/08-three-attacks-and-the-wall.md.

The construction

The absolute object is not a scheme of finite type over a number field. The candidate is a fibered polarized Hodge-index realization

\[ \pi:\mathcal{H}\longrightarrow \mathcal{S}, \]

where \(\mathcal{S}\) is the Scaling Site and the fibre over the periodic orbit at \(p\) is the abelian surface

\[ \mathcal{H}_p = E_p\times E_p, \qquad E_p=\mathbb{C}\Big/\Bigl(\mathbb{Z}+\frac{\log p}{2\pi i}\mathbb{Z}\Bigr). \]
  • Fibrewise. \(\operatorname{NS}(\mathcal{H}_p)\) has the rank-three

basis \(H,V,\Delta\), with Gram matrix \[ \begin{pmatrix}0&1&1\\1&0&1\\1&1&0\end{pmatrix}. \] The ample class \(A=H+V+\Delta\) satisfies \(A^2=6\), and the Hodge index \(6D^2\le(A\cdot D)^2\) holds. This is the Lean model T5–T9 of ../intersection-form-zeta/lean/Topic.lean.

  • Globally. The fibrewise pairings are assembled by the transverse measure

of the foliation; the resulting pairing is the CCM trace \(Z(f)\cdot Z(f)=s(f,f)\). The assembly is the longitudinal Hirzebruch–Riemann–Roch formula on \(\mathcal{H}\), which remains open.

Rescue status (round 45, historical)

The objective is not achieved, and the dossier is not closed. Round 45 found that the round-44 assessment mistook a normalization error for a mathematical wall:

  • The recorded symbol satisfies the exact identity

\(M(\xi)=A_{\text{arch}}(\xi)+Q(\xi)=-Z'/Z(-i\xi)\) on \(\operatorname{Im}\xi>1\). It is the completed-zeta symbol on the imaginary axis, not on the critical line. The \(n^{-1/2}\) of the explicit formula is exactly the contour shift \(\sigma\mapsto\sigma+\frac12\).

  • The recorded functional is indefinite on exact mean-zero

compact-support tests (smallest eigenvalue \(-1.7\cdot10^{-3}\), stable to eight digits across quadrature orders). This is not a truncation artefact. A single relative rescaling of its two halves also fails.

  • On the certified four-Gaussian basis, the recorded \(N\) has eigenvalues

\((-0.0408,-0.0195,-0.0173,+0.0624)\), while the correct \(W\) has \((0.0227,0.0270,0.0449,0.0780)\), reproducing ../prime-spectrum-tensor/WEIL-GRAM.md. The sibling's \(+\Omega\) functional passes the narrow-Gaussian test but fails the broad compact-support test. The standard \(-\Omega\) formula is near-positive on broad supports (smallest eigenvalue \(-4.4\cdot10^{-3}\), or \(-2.7\cdot10^{-4}\) on a smooth bump); the residual is the open problem of note 39.

  • The pointwise sign problem of notes 25–27 is ill-posed: the prime half is a

measure on the log-lattice, not a boundary value of a meromorphic function.

  • The uniform-bound and relative-form obstructions of notes 34–36 are

withdrawn: the inductive limit needs compatibility, not a uniform bound.

  • The sign convention is resolved by \(s=-W\): the geometric self-intersection

is the negative of the Weil functional.

The corrected objective is Weil positivity for the correct \(W\) (pole integral, \(n^{-1/2}\) prime sum, and the \(\Omega\) archimedean density), with the finite Gram certificates as acceptance tests. See notes/37-final-assessment.md and notes/38-rescue.md. No sign claim about the CCM form should be based on the pointwise symbol, and no RH proof is claimed yet.

Three candidates have been examined:

  1. Bost–Connes endomotive / modular tower. The profinite tower

\(\varprojlim X_0(N)\) (equivalently \(C^*(\mathbb{Q}/\mathbb{Z})\rtimes\mathbb{N}^\times\)) carries the Hecke correspondences, the Petersson/Hodge–Riemann polarization, and the KMS half-weight \(p^{-k/2}\). It is the strongest candidate for the global metrized object. The finite-level Hodge–Riemann positivity is checked; the transverse \(\log p\) length is the missing theorem. See notes/02-endomotive-candidate.md.

The two algebraic candidates below fail for proved reasons:

  1. Hecke/Shimura surface. \(X_0(N)\times X_0(N)\) is proper and polarized

over \(\mathbb{Q}\), and the Hecke correspondences \(T_p\) are algebraic; the Hodge index applies. But the trace is the Hecke eigenvalue \(a_p\), not the CCM local term \(\frac{\log p}{p^{k/2}}(f(p^k)+f(p^{-k}))\). The dictionary fails.

  1. Scaling Site square. The complex lift

\(\Gamma_\mathbb{Q}^2\) carries the correct pairing, but it is a foliated complex space, not a variety of finite type over a number field. Yuan–Zhang does not apply.
