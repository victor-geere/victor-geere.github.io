HST transfer operator — O5 finite reconstruction

Parent programme: harmonic-sine-transform · Topic index: topics

Status. Finite reconstruction and exact computation layer. The greedy map, inverse branches, archived two-branch operator, finite truncations, infinite limit, linearity and operator bounds are Lean-proved. Trace-classness, the Fredholm determinant and the determinant identity are not proved. No RH or GRH claim is made.

Sources: harmonic-sine-transform.tex and hst-further-research-results.tex. The topic isolates the O5 operator programme left open by the parent dossier.

Source defects carried in

  • R17: two different L_s descriptions; neither is derived from the digit map. The finite formula here is the archived two-branch sum, not a canonical operator.
  • R18: the source’s displayed T uses scaling (n+2)/(n+1) and threshold 1/(n+2); the actual normalised remainder uses (n+3)/(n+2) and threshold 1. At n=0 the source map sends 1 to 1, the actual map sends 1 to 0.

Cross-topic support and R14

Authorised future O7 support (not O5 theorems): greedy-harmonic-hilbert-polya T1–T7, harmonic-categorical-hilbert-polya T1–T6, and self-adjoint-ccm T8–T11 (whose constant-matching spectrum is a lattice and is excluded).

R14 resolved: the greedy–MTC “bijection” passage is a research question; the results paper proves no literal bijection exists while preserving branching. The categorical branch may use only the transfer-operator route.

What is not formalised

  • Trace-classness on a Hardy/Banach space.
  • The Fredholm determinant and det(I − L_s) = ζ(s)/ζ(2s)eP(s).
  • Meromorphic continuation to Re s > 1/2.
  • The explicit formula and weights.
  • Any self-adjoint or Hilbert–Pólya statement.
