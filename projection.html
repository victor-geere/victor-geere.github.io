The chart

The unit sphere \(S^2\subset\mathbb{R}^3\) has north pole \(N=(0,0,1)\). A ray from \(N\) through a point \(P=(x,y,z)\neq N\) meets the equatorial plane \(z=0\) at \(\pi_N(P)\). Identify that plane with \(\mathbb{C}\) by \(u+iv\).

\[ \pi_N(x,y,z)=\frac{x+iy}{1-z},\qquad \pi_N^{-1}(w)=\Bigl(\frac{2w}{1+\lvert w\rvert^2},\;\frac{\lvert w\rvert^2-1}{1+\lvert w\rvert^2}\Bigr) \] in \(\mathbb{C}\times\mathbb{R}\). The south pole \(S=(0,0,-1)\) is the origin. The north pole is the point at infinity.

Stereographic projection is conformal and sends circles to circles or lines. Meridians (great circles through \(N\)) become straight lines through \(0\). Parallels become concentric circles. A rhumb line, meeting meridians at constant bearing, becomes a logarithmic spiral.

The nested-spheres globe in viz.html is the same \(S^2\) fibre, hinged at the origin. Flattening it from the north pole is this chart. The flatten slider moves points along the projecting rays.
